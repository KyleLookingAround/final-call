# Version history

Newest first. The version numbers match the published claude.ai artifact.

| Version | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 27 | **A smarter transport manager.** The manager runs your region lines by what each change is worth to the network and the airport: how often they run, fares and meeting flights. It adds services at once to a line so full it costs rating, and runs extra services to the venue while event crowds travel. It suggests upgrading a line to a tram, train, metro or high-speed rail, extending one by a station, or closing one other lines now do better, each with its value, payback and a preview on the map. Any line can be upgraded from its card, and keeps running until the new one opens. |
| 26 | **Mobile lounges.** With the Remote apron, buy mobile lounges on stilts, as at Washington Dulles: they drive out to the remote stands and rise to the door, so remote boarding is as quick as a bridge in any weather and costs no rating. Every layout is now a real 2D shape. |
| 25 | **More real airports.** The Satellite becomes Heathrow Terminal 5: the main building and two satellites out on the apron, reached by an underground train. The Starfish becomes Beijing Daxing: five piers round a star-shaped hall, entered along the sixth spoke. Two new layouts: Midfield concourses (from level 9), with sixteen stands along a train like Atlanta and Denver; and the Round terminal (from level 6), a round building with glass tubes across its middle and satellite pods reached through tunnels, like Paris Charles de Gaulle Terminal 1. |
| 24 | **Real airport shapes.** Four layouts are rebuilt as real shapes. The Remote apron puts its stands out on the open apron, with buses from gates at the end of the concourse. The Staggered apron is a herringbone pier with planes parked at an angle. The Curved front is one long curved building with planes fanned round it and moving walkways along it, like Osaka Kansai. The Hall and finger pier fans three piers out from a central hall of shops, like Amsterdam Schiphol. The Satellite and Starfish follow. |
| 23 | **Classic, made real.** Planes park nose-in with short jet bridges, as at real airports, and push back when they leave. Pier B is now a pier out onto the apron with gates on both sides, reached along the concourse, with the people mover running out along it. A control tower stands by the gates. This starts real airport shapes; the other layouts follow. |
| 22 | **Airport layouts.** Unlock six new airport layouts in the Masterplan and rebuild into them from Airfield › Layout: Remote apron, Staggered apron, Curved front, Hall and finger pier, Satellite and Starfish, the later ones modelled on real airports. Each trades stands, shops, walks and running costs differently; remote stands board by bus. Fast speeds run more smoothly on phones. A What's new page opens after updates and from Settings or Help, with the full history. Sharing the link shows a preview card. |
| 21 | **Mobile fixes.** The bottom panel slides over the map while you drag it, so the map no longer jumps or redraws at every step. Snap sizes always leave some map in view. The full-screen drawer hides properly and opens from Manage. The page can no longer be scrolled or bounced on phones. Landscape phones show the map and panel side by side, and the camera space moves to the sides. The tip hides when the map is very short, and the bottom bar no longer runs under Manage in full screen. |
| 20 | **Five features.** Lowmere rival airport; crews, overnight checks and far-end delays; Records with stamps and weekly challenges; a guided first hour; saves across devices on claude.ai. Also: the top buttons wrap on 320 px phones, and there are two new plans (Slot agreements, Operations centre). |
| 19 | **Space for the phone camera** (Settings › Screen, None to Large; Medium by default on phones). The full-screen cash chip no longer covers the top buttons, the "Paused" tag no longer turns into an empty box, and stand labels no longer overlap. |
| 18 | **Recommendations and managers.** The Region and Routes tabs suggest lines, routes, fares and planes. Transport, route and (later) fleet managers run the details and step back when you change something yourself. |
| 17 | **Settings** to turn off tips, messages, map pop-ups, the goal bar, recommendations and badges; replies to your own taps always show. |
| 16 | **Cohesion and progression overhaul.** Ten levels, from Airfield to Airport of the Year. A Masterplan tech tree bought with plan points. A world map of routes with city markets and fares. Travellers as business, leisure, families, groups and passengers needing assistance. Layout work for phones, tablets and large screens. |
| ≤15 | The airport, boarding methods, the region with transport and development, weather and events, and more. Older saves still load. |

## Design rules that have held throughout

- No choice of scenario at the start; everyone starts at the same airfield.
- Locked things are hidden, not greyed out.
- Changes show on the map, board and gates, not as text events.
- Text is concise.
- Balance is checked with the bot over about 1,150 game hours, and the headless run must keep working.
