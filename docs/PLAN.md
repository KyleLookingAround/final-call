## 1. Rival airport at Lowmere

When you become a City Airport, Lowmere starts building an airport. It opens three days later and competes with you for travellers on the routes you both fly.

- **How it competes.** On a shared route, travellers split between you by frequency, fare, rating and on-time record. A neglected route can lose about 40% of its travellers; a well-run one keeps most of them.
- **How it grows.** Once a day Lowmere adds a route or a flight. It favours routes where your planes are full or your service is weak. Sometimes it runs a three-day fare sale on one of your best routes.
- **How it backs off.** On routes where you keep more than 80% of travellers for four days, Lowmere cuts a flight or leaves the route.
- **How you fight back.** Fly more often, price sharper, keep flights on time and keep your rating up. Two plans help:
  - Slot agreements (a Masterplan plan) give you an edge and slow Lowmere's growth.
  - High-speed rail to Lowmere brings its catchment to you.
  
  At World Gateway you can buy Lowmere outright. It then stops competing and pays you a daily share of its traffic.
- **What you see:**
  - a construction site, then an airport with planes crossing your sky on the region map;
  - dashed purple routes on the world map;
  - a share bar on each route card and a Lowmere card on the Routes tab;
  - "Defend" recommendations.

## 2. Airline operations

Your own planes now need crews, can be checked overnight, and can come back late.

- **Crews with duty hours.** Every departure of your own aircraft needs a rested crew.
  - A crew flies the round trip. After about 10 hours on duty it rests for 12.
  - With no crew free, the flight waits at the gate as CREW DELAY, which makes it late and also makes the plane late back.
  - Partner airlines bring their own crews.
- **Hiring.** Crews are hired from Gates › Fleet, with a hiring fee and hourly wages; both are close to zero early on. The fleet manager keeps enough crews and can be turned off.
- **Overnight checks.** A Maintenance policy, on by default, services worn planes parked at 03:00 at a discount. A maintenance hangar makes it cheaper.
- **Knock-on delays.** Weather and slots at the far end can bring planes back late, which is more likely in winter and on long-haul.
  - The world map shows the late plane in red, and the Fleet panel shows how late it is.
  - A late plane leaves its gate idle, so spare planes or partners fill in.
  - An Operations centre plan halves these delays.
- **On-time record.** The daily on-time rate feeds your share against Lowmere.

## 3. Records, stamps and weekly challenges

A Records page in the Office holds three things:

- **Records.** Busiest day, best day, most punctual day, longest on-time run, most destinations, highest rating, busiest public transport hour and best share against Lowmere. Breaking one shows a gold RECORD marker.
- **Stamps.** 24 stamps; only earned ones are shown, with a count of how many are left to find.
- **Weekly challenges.** Three challenges each game week, sized from last week (about 12–15% above it). Each pays about 12% of a day's profit. Finishing all three gives a plan point while unapproved plans remain, and cash after that. Challenges can be switched off in Settings.

## 4. Guided first hour

New games start with a six-step tour. A ring highlights the real control and waits for you to use it:

1. See where passengers arrive.
2. Tap gate A1.
3. Speed up time.
4. Open a second check-in desk.
5. Get a flight away on time.
6. Read the goals and tips.

The tour can be skipped at any step and replayed from Help. It never starts on existing saves or in headless runs.

## 5. Saves across devices

When the game runs as an artifact on claude.ai, it also saves to the player's own private space in their account:

- It saves every minute of play and whenever the page is hidden.
- On opening, it uses whichever save is newer. If both devices moved on, it asks which to keep.
- Anywhere else, including GitHub Pages, the game saves on the device only. The save code in Settings moves an airport between copies.

## Checks before release (results)

In two 1,150-hour bot runs, the bot reached Airport of the Year at 1,110 and 1,083 game hours, against 1,027–1,113 before. There were no errors, and one run bought Lowmere on day 46. Old saves load and play, and no screen from 320 to 2560 px overflows.
