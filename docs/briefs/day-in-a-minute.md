The owner starts sharing the game on 28 Sep and asked overnight for "some cool updates when I wake up", and not to wait on them. This idea is from the owner's ranked idea board (`docs/ideas/board-2026-09.md`, "The owner's order"), where they rated it Love it (third in their order). ## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/day-in-a-minute` from `main`, in a new file `src/game/63-day-in-a-minute.js`.
- Board: "A time-lapse of yesterday at your airport, played back over the map at high speed." Record a light sample of the day as it runs (every few game minutes: planes on stands and the runway, passenger counts per hall, the queue lengths, cars and trains in the region if cheap), in `R` only (runtime; kept in a small ring buffer, not saved, so it starts after a reload). At the end of each day, a "Yesterday in a minute" button (in the day report or the Office) plays it back over the airport view in about 60 seconds: the lighting runs dawn to night, planes appear and leave at their stands, crowd heat shows in the halls, and a clock and the day's headline numbers (flights, passengers, on-time, earnings) tick along the top. Tap to stop. Photo mode may be merging alongside: if its time-of-day override exists, reuse it.
- Recording must never change the game: read only, no `rnd()`, nothing in `G`. Nothing is recorded in `R.sim`.
- Checks in a new file `tools/checks/day-in-a-minute.mjs`: after a seeded day the recording holds samples across the day within its memory cap; playback draws each frame without throwing at phone and desktop sizes; `G` and the random stream are unchanged by recording or playback.
- It may touch: the new file, one registration line in the clock tables and the day report (`records` or `15-panel.js`), `50-scene.js` only to read a playback override, `src/shell.html` for the controls, the new check, `src/updates.d/day-in-a-minute.md`, `docs/specs/day-in-a-minute.md`, `docs/systems/day-in-a-minute.md`, its look back, and this brief as `docs/briefs/day-in-a-minute.md`.
