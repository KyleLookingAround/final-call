## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/famous-faces` from `main`, in a new file `src/game/64-famous-faces.js`, hooked in through the clock tables and the passenger tables rather than editing shared loops.
- Board: "Now and then someone famous flies through: photographers at arrivals, fans at the barrier, and a busy hour at the café they sit in." Every few game days (rarer at low levels, from level 3), a named celebrity (an invented name and a kind: footballer, pop star, film actor, royal; never a real person) is booked on one of your flights, shown a day ahead on the board with a star and in the region news. On the day: a cluster of photographers at arrivals or check-in, fans gathered at the landside barrier, a busy hour at the café or shop they visit (a bump in spend there through `effect()`), and a small rating bonus if their flight leaves on time, a small hit if it's delayed. Late runners (#99's PR) adds a tap-a-passenger card: if it has merged, the celebrity's card says who they are.
- Pacing: a small, occasional boost; report the bot's level hours on seeds 1–3 against `tools/baseline.json`, within the 15% tolerance. All chance through `rnd()`.
- Checks in a new file `tools/checks/famous-faces.mjs`: over a seeded run a visit is announced a day ahead, the crowd appears and clears, spend and rating effects go through the ledger with their place, nothing throws in `R.sim`, and old saves load (any saved field has its line in `FIELDS`).
- It may touch: the new file, one registration line each where a table needs it (`07-passengers.js`, `14-board.js`, `46-market.js`, the region news), `src/shell.html` for any styling, `03-state.js` (`FIELDS`, one line if a saved field is needed), the new check, `src/updates.d/famous-faces.md`, `docs/specs/famous-faces.md`, `docs/systems/famous-faces.md`, its look back, and this brief as `docs/briefs/famous-faces.md`.
