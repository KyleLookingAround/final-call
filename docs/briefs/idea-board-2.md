# Brief: A new idea board for every part of the game

The owner's brief for a second idea board (round 3 of the owner's ratings): ideas across every part of Final Call to rate and comment on, with the outcome recorded in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. No game code.

## Goal and what it may touch

- Publish a new idea board as a claude.ai artifact, like the owner's earlier "Final Call Idea Board" (https://claude.ai/artifact/SSFu3CuvyNKGttJDoirVTf): ideas across every part of Final Call that the owner can rate and comment on, and whose ratings and comments are saved so the session can read them back. Then, once the owner has rated them, record the outcome in the repo. Branch `feature/idea-board-2` from `main`, one PR.
- **The board:**
  - Read the old board first (`Artifact` `read`) and follow how it worked: ratings, comments, grouping. Keep what worked and give it the same look. It's a new artifact at a new URL; don't change the old one.
  - Load the `artifact-design` and `artifact-capabilities` skills before writing it. Keep ratings and comments in a capability that saves shared state, not browser storage, so you can read them back. It must work on a phone.
  - Cover **every part of the game**, with 40–60 ideas in groups:
    - the airport view and building;
    - flights, airlines, fleet and routes;
    - passengers;
    - the terminal;
    - the region and transport;
    - the world map;
    - Lowmere and rivals;
    - progression, levels and the Masterplan;
    - economy and managers;
    - events and stakes;
    - UI, phone and accessibility;
    - sound and look;
    - onboarding and help;
    - long-term play and replay;
    - community and sharing.
  - Each idea gets a title, one or two plain sentences on what the player would notice, a rough size (small, medium or large) and the bundle it would fit, if any.
  - Ground ideas in the game as it is: the project notes, `docs/SYSTEMS.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md` and a quick play in `build/test.html`. #57's game-logic ideas (`docs/ideas/game-logic.md` on branch `feature/game-logic-ideas`, or on `main` once merged) go in as their own group, marked as coming from there, so the owner rates everything in one place.
  - Leave out what's already built, in flight or decided:
    - in flight: the five real-airport parts, "The terminal as a place" (#49), the Ko-fi and feedback links;
    - rejected: placing decor, a share price, eras, a sandbox;
    - parked: seasons and winter, music. Parked ideas may show in a small "parked" corner the owner can revive.
  - Respect the owner's preferences in the project notes. UK English.
- **After the owner rates:**
  - When you publish, give the owner the link and say you'll read the ratings when they tell you they're done.
  - Then subscribe to the PR you'll open and end your turn. The coordinator will also relay "done".
  - When they're done, read the saved ratings and comments. Update `docs/ROADMAP.md`:
    - loved and liked ideas go under "Ideas (not agreed)", in the owner's order;
    - anything they reject goes under "Rejected", and anything parked under "Parked";
    - the owner's comments, where they change an idea, go in its line.
  - Save the board's raw results as `docs/ideas/board-2026-09.md`, with this brief as `docs/briefs/idea-board-2.md` and a look back in `docs/LESSONS.md`.
  - Don't reorder the owner's bundles or start any work; the owner decides what moves up.
- Files it may touch: `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/ideas/board-2026-09.md`, `docs/briefs/idea-board-2.md` and `docs/LESSONS.md`, plus the new artifact. No game code. Anything else is outside the brief.
