## Goal and what it may touch

- A small, quiet link styled as a button, reading "Buy me a Ko-fi" with a small cup. It opens `https://ko-fi.com/kylemck` in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener"`). It appears in three places:
  - **What's new:** at the foot of the page.
  - **Office › Settings:** at the foot, under "Your save".
  - **The level-up card:** one line at the foot, under the links to what's unlocked and never above them. It must not change the card's pausing, its speed restore or how it closes.

The page stays one file with no dependencies (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-26-one-page-no-dependencies.md`), so draw the cup inline (an SVG or text).
- It follows the game's own look and the dark theme, and fits a 320 px phone, a landscape phone, a tablet and a large screen. The level-up card still fits a 320 px phone with the line added (the `levelup` check covers this; keep it passing).
