## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/late-runners` from `main`, in a new file `src/game/61-late-runners.js` hooked into the passenger and gate tables (`PAX_STEP`, the clock tables) rather than editing shared loops.
- **Late runners** (board: "Passengers still shopping at final call run to the gate. The gate holds a minute or closes its door, and the board shows GATE CLOSING."): stragglers visibly run (faster drawn pace, eased as `movement` expects), the gate holds briefly or closes, the board's flaps show GATE CLOSING, and a runner who misses the flight is a small rating cause through `effect()` with its stand. Uses `rnd()` for anything that changes state.
- **The owner's note on it:** "a rim world style page for a passenger when you tap on them so you can see where they have been and their emotion as a story". Tap a passenger (on the airport view) for a small card: who they are (name, from where, going where, why), a short timeline of where they've been today (arrived by train at 07:12, checked in, security queue 9 min, coffee in the market place, ran to gate 14) and a mood that rises and falls with what happened, written as a few plain sentences. Keep what's saved small: the story lives on the runtime passenger (`R.pax`), not in `G`.
- Pacing: the owner allows the game to feel longer if there's always something to do; this shouldn't slow levels. `PLAY` may change (runners change timings): report the bot's level hours on seeds 1–3 against `tools/baseline.json`, within the 15% tolerance.
- Checks in a new file `tools/checks/late-runners.mjs`: a runner appears when a straggler is shopping at final call, runs, and either boards or misses; the board shows GATE CLOSING; tapping a passenger opens the card with a timeline; nothing throws in `R.sim`.
- It may touch: the new file, one registration line each where a table needs it (`07-passengers.js`, `08-stands.js`, `14-board.js`, `46-market.js`), `src/shell.html` for the card's CSS, the new check, `src/updates.d/late-runners.md`, `docs/specs/late-runners.md`, `docs/systems/late-runners.md`, its look back, and this brief as `docs/briefs/late-runners.md`.
