# Brief: Launch polish audit

The owner starts posting and sharing the game from 28 Sep. Overnight they asked to keep polishing: how it looks, how it feels, the menus (moving things where they'd be easier to find), balance, anything. This audit finds what a newcomer arriving from a shared link would notice first; fix sessions follow from its issues.

## Goal and what it may touch

- A ranked punch list of what makes Final Call feel unfinished **to a first-time player arriving from a shared link**, found by playing and looking. One PR on `feature/launch-audit` from `main`, docs only, plus GitHub issues.
- Play and look at, at phone (390×844 and 320×568, `hasTouch`, `isMobile`, portrait and landscape), tablet (768×1024) and desktop (1440×900), by day and night:
  - a **new game** from a clean `localStorage`: the first screen, the guided start, the first 30 minutes of play at 1× and 4×, the first level-up;
  - the ready-made airports at levels 3, 5 and 9 (`tools/saves/`): every panel tab, the menus, Settings, Help, What's new, the Masterplan, the board, the Region and the world map;
  - what a shared link shows: the page title, description, link preview image and icon (`npm run preview`, `docs/SYSTEMS.md` "Link previews").
- Look for:
  - **menus**: things hard to find, in the wrong tab, too deep, or shown before they matter; propose where each should move (the owner asked for this);
  - **feel**: taps with no response, numbers that jump, missing sound or motion on the moments that matter (first flight, a build finishing, a level-up), anything that happens off-screen with no pointer;
  - **looks**: inconsistent cards, buttons, spacing, fonts, colours, clipping, text too small, anything at 320 px, the phone camera band;
  - **first hour pacing**: dead time, confusion, too much text at once;
  - console errors and slow frames.
- Skip what's already being worked on: the region map's looks (its own session tonight), the terminal as a place (groundwork running), and the rating/network balance (#96). Read `docs/ideas/polish-2026-09.md` and don't repeat rows already fixed (#84, #87, #88, #92, #80).
- Write `docs/ideas/polish-launch.md`: a table ranked by how much a newcomer notices it against how small the fix is; each row has where it is, what's wrong, the fix in a sentence, its size, the files it would touch, and a screenshot name. Group rows into fix batches of 3–5 that touch the same files, **with no two batches sharing a file**, and mark which would collide with the terminal-place groundwork (`docs/briefs/terminal-place-groundwork.md` lists its files) or the region map session (`24`–`30`).
- Open one GitHub issue per batch, labelled `polish`, listing its rows, biggest first. Fix nothing in this session.
- It may touch: `docs/ideas/polish-launch.md`, one roadmap item in `docs/roadmap.d/`, its look back in `docs/lessons/`, and this brief as `docs/briefs/launch-audit.md`. Scripts and screenshots stay in `build/`.
