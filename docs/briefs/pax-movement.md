# Brief: Passengers who suddenly speed down the piers

The owner saw passengers walk to a spot and then move very fast down the terminal. A fix to how movement looks and feels, and to where it's wrong.

## Goal and what it may touch

- First open a `bug` issue (Bug template) with what you find, then one PR on `feature/pax-movement` from `main` that closes it.
- **Likely cause, to confirm first:** `walkMul` (`src/game/07-passengers.js:45`) multiplies a walker's speed by 2.5 once the people mover is bought, whenever the passenger has a route (`p.way`) or a target more than 300 px away across, while still drawing them as a walker; the mover's car on its track (`12-drawing.js`, `LAY.track`) moves on its own and carries no one. Moving-walkway links double the speed again on top (`walk` in `src/game/41-airside.js`, `m===2`), and `LAY.xfer` and a room's `walk` stack too. Measure it: log each passenger's speed per step over a busy hour on `tools/saves/v32-L9.json` (Classic with the mover, and a layout with walkways and a train), and list every place speed jumps by more than about 1.5× from one step to the next, and why.
- **Fix it so movement reads true:** nobody changes speed without a visible reason. A passenger sped up by the mover is seen riding it (hidden and drawn as the car, as trains already are, boarding and leaving at its ends), or walks at walking pace; on a walkway link they're on a drawn walkway at a steady speed; speed changes ease over a fraction of a second rather than jumping; and no multiplier stacks on another by accident. Also look for any other sudden jumps you measure (queues catching up, a target reset mid-walk, targets snapping at a doorway).
- **The game must play the same:** keep each journey's total time as it is, so `PLAY` stays identical on seeds 1–3 (the Balance workflow shows it). If a true fix can't keep it (for example, routing riders along the track changes walk times), keep it within `tools/baseline.json`'s tolerance, say exactly what moved in the PR, and prefer the version that leaves `PLAY` identical.
- Check first, in a new group `tools/checks/movement.mjs`: over a seeded hour, no passenger drawn as walking moves faster than a set walking top speed, and no step's speed exceeds the one before by more than a set factor, except at a named, drawn change (boarding a train or mover, stepping on a walkway). It fails on `main` for the reason above.
- Look at it moving: short screen recordings or frame strips at 1× and 4× on desktop and a 390 px phone, before and after, in the PR.
- It may touch: `src/game/07-passengers.js` (`walkMul`), `walk` and `route` in `src/game/41-airside.js`, how passengers and the mover are drawn in `src/game/12-drawing.js` and `src/game/40-layout-drawing.js`, the walk calls in `43`, `44` and `46` (speed arguments only), `tools/checks/movement.mjs` (new), `docs/SYSTEMS.md` (a short note on how walking speed is set), `docs/LESSONS.md` and this brief as `docs/briefs/pax-movement.md`. Bug #78 may touch `41-airside.js` near `switchLayout`'s needs; keep to `walk`/`route`. Not `switchLayout`, not the terminal-place checks' files.
