## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/photo-mode` from `main`, in a new file `src/game/62-photo-mode.js`.
- Board: "Hide the panels, pick the time of day and frame your airport for a picture." A camera button (in the view controls, reachable on a phone) enters photo mode: every panel, chip, toast and the phone chrome hide; a small bar offers time of day (dawn, noon, dusk, night, drawn only: `G.clock` and the simulation don't change), weather look (clear, rain, fog, snow, drawn only), pause, and a shutter that saves a PNG of the canvas with a small "Final Call" mark in a corner (a download on desktop, the share sheet on phones where `navigator.share` with files works). Tap or Esc to leave. It works in the airport view and the Region.
- Nothing it does may change `G` or the random stream: the drawn time and weather are overrides the scene reads, cleared on leaving.
- Checks in a new file `tools/checks/photo-mode.mjs`: entering hides every panel at phone, tablet and desktop sizes; each time of day draws without throwing; the shutter makes a PNG of the canvas size; leaving restores the panels; `G` and the random stream are unchanged.
- It may touch: the new file, `50-scene.js` (reading the overrides; one or two lines), `54-weather.js` (reading the weather override; one or two lines), `21-layout.js` or `src/shell.html` for the button and bar, the new check, `src/updates.d/photo-mode.md`, `docs/specs/photo-mode.md`, `docs/systems/photo-mode.md`, its look back, and this brief as `docs/briefs/photo-mode.md`.
