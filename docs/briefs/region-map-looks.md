# Brief: The region map, looking better

The owner starts sharing the game from 28 Sep and asked overnight for the region map to look better. It's a restyle of what's already drawn, not a new feature: nothing about how the region plays changes.

## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/region-map-looks` from `main` that makes the Region view look good enough to screenshot and share, at every level and zoom, by day and night, on phone and desktop.
- Start by looking: screenshot the Region at levels 3, 5 and 9 (`tools/saves/`), at 390×844, 768×1024 and 1440×900, zoomed out and in, at noon, dusk and night, in rain and fog. Write down what looks unfinished (flat land, the coast, towns as boxes, lines and stations, labels, the airport, clutter at zoom-out, night) and pick the handful of changes that lift it most. A cohesive map style, consistent with the airport view's palette, beats many small effects.
- Likely candidates, for you to judge from the screenshots: land with gentle relief or texture and a softer coastline with shallows; towns that read as built-up areas that grow with population; lines styled like a transit map (clean joins, casing, parallel lines side by side); stations and interchanges that read at a glance; labels that don't collide and scale with zoom; lights on at night; the airport as a recognisable shape.
- Keep it all in drawing: no change to game state, `rnd()` untouched (cosmetic randomness only on lines ending `// cosmetic`, or seeded as `seeded()` already does). Cache anything static (terrain, coast) to an offscreen canvas rebuilt only when the view or the towns change.
- Add a check in a new file `tools/checks/region-looks.mjs`: drawing the Region at each of the three saves changes nothing in `G` and throws nothing, labels stay inside the view at 320 px, and the Region's frame time is within the budget below.
- It may touch: `src/game/29-region-map.js`, `30-region-ui.js` (the legend and controls only), place data in `24-region-places.js` only if the look needs a shape it lacks (no saved field changes), the new check, `docs/systems/region.md`, link previews if the Region appears in them, its look back in `docs/lessons/`, and this brief as `docs/briefs/region-map-looks.md`.
- **Refactor 8** (weather in one place, running now) converts `R.fx` reads to `wx.on(kind)` in `29-region-map.js`'s weather drawing. Leave those lines as they are; if refactor 8 merges first, use `wx.on`.
