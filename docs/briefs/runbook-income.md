# Brief: Something new that earns, built with the runbook

The owner asked on 28 Sep 2026 for a session that uses the runbook (the way this repo runs sessions: briefs, playbooks, checks, a coordinator, look backs) to build something else that could earn an income quickly with little input from the owner. This session researches and proposes. It builds nothing until the owner picks an option.

## Goal and what it may touch

- Write a proposal, `docs/proposals/runbook-income.md`, with three to five options, each: what it is, who pays and why, how the runbook builds and runs it, what the owner must do by hand (accounts, payments, tax, anything legal), time to first sale, realistic takings with sources, running cost in Claude usage, and risks. Be honest about "quickly with little input": say what can't be automated.
- Options to weigh include, but aren't limited to: selling the runbook itself as a template or guide (the runbook pack, `docs/briefs/runbook-pack.md`, is a start); small paid browser games or tools made the same way as Final Call; a service built on the runbook for other people's repos. Rule out anything that needs ads, spam, scraping, impersonation, or that would breach a platform's or Anthropic's terms, and say why it's ruled out.
- Recommend one, with a first-week plan: the new repo it needs (the owner creates it), its project notes and first briefs, and what the owner does and when. Draft those project notes and first brief as appendices so the owner can start it in one step.
- Branch `feature/runbook-income` from `main`, one draft PR. It may touch: `docs/proposals/runbook-income.md` (new), this brief as `docs/briefs/runbook-income.md`, and its own `docs/lessons/` file. Nothing in `src/`, the playbooks or the checks.
