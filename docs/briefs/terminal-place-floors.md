# Brief: Two floors in Classic (a part of "The terminal as a place")

The coordinator's brief for one part of `docs/specs/terminal-place.md` (approved on #48), in the first batch with windows and watchers, and decor and local character. Start it as a fresh session with this file as its first message, on a branch from `main` after the groundwork (#GROUNDWORK) has merged.

## Goal and what it may touch

- Stack Classic's halls on two floors, as the spec's "What they see" and "How it works" describe: departures upstairs (`ci`, `sec`, `mkt`, `fl` 1), arrivals below (`imm`, `rec`, `cus`, `arh`, the baggage hall, `wlk`, `fl` 0), no two rooms on one floor overlapping, with escalators and a lift between them as floor links in `LAYOUTS.classic.term.doors` (`[hall, hall, x, y, half-width, 'esc' or 'lift', people a minute]`). Passengers queue for a floor link like a door, `p.famL` passengers take the lift, and bags never use floor links (the belts drop to the baggage hall). Tapping a hall's name, an advisor tip or a board line flies the camera there and switches `R.floor` to its floor (`hallLabel(id)` gives where a hall's name is drawn). Old saves put each passenger on their hall's floor, or at the nearest doorway if the hall moved.
- Only Classic moves. The other layouts keep their copy of today's plan until the floor plans part.
- Counters, lanes, booths, carousels and queues that move with a hall read their places from `L.term` (a table of where each part's counters sit), not from constants in `43`–`47`; the groundwork left them as constants because nothing moved.
- Switch on its pending checks by taking their lines out of `tools/checks/pending.txt`: the seven `floors:` checks and `scene: one floor at a time`. Say in the PR how each failed on `main` and passes now.
- Branch `feature/terminal-place-floors`, one PR, labelled `part:terminal-place`.
- Files and hooks it may touch: a new `src/game/56-floors.js` (floor links' queues, the lift, old saves, hall taps); `LAYOUTS.classic.term` (in `42-terminal.js`, where `classicTerm()` builds every layout's copy: give Classic its own); the places in `43`–`47` that read the table; `drawRooms` in `40-layout-drawing.js` and the parts' `TERM_DRAW` entries, so each draws only its floor (`onFloor(fl)`, `floorNow()` in `53-roofs.js`); `route`/`walk` in `41-airside.js` only if a floor link's queue needs it; `tools/checks/floors.mjs` only to fix a mistake (say what and why); `docs/systems/terminal.md` and a new `docs/systems/floors.md`. Anything else is outside the brief.
- Screenshots of Classic's two floors at phone (390×844 and 320×568), tablet (768×1024) and desktop (1440×900), by day and night, zoomed out and at 1.6×, looked at before the PR opens.
