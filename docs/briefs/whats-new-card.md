# Brief: What's new, easier to read and act on

Issue #128, raised by the owner on 28 Sep 2026. Release 35 (six merged changes, the biggest card yet) starts no earlier than 29 Sep 09:00 UTC, so this should merge before then.

## Goal and what it may touch

- One PR on `feature/whats-new-card` from `main`, closing #128. It makes the What's new card scan quickly and lets a player jump to what changed. Four of the issue's ideas, taken as the default:
  1. **Shorter points.** Each point is a bold lead of a few words and one short sentence. Rewrite the existing `UPDATES` entries' text to match (text only, never the version numbers), and set the style for new fragments in `src/updates.d/README.md`.
  2. **"Show me".** A point can name a target (a tab, a panel, a stand or a place on the map). Its small button closes the card and goes there, the way the level-up card's links land on the right tab. Points without a target show no button.
  3. **Hide what's locked.** A point can carry the level it needs. The card hides points the save hasn't reached (the owner's rule: hide locked items instead of greying them out). A version whose points are all hidden folds into the "N earlier versions" summary.
  4. **Phone fit.** At 320×568 and 568×320 the newest entry and the Play button are in reach without scrolling past Play; tablets and desktops stay as they are or better.
  - Idea 4 of the issue (a picture per version) is left out: note it in the spec as a later step.
- **The spec comes first.** Update `docs/specs/whats-new.md` (from `docs/specs/TEMPLATE.md` if it needs reshaping) as the first commit, open the PR as a draft at once, and post its link. The owner approves specs; carry on building while they read it, and follow any comment they leave on the PR.
- `UPDATES` entries gain optional fields only (a target and a level per point); old entries keep working without them. No saved state changes beyond what `G.seen` already does. It changes no rules: `PLAY` and `STATE` identical on seeds 1–3 against `main`.
- It may touch: `src/game/38-updates.js`, the What's new card's CSS in `src/shell.html` (`#news`, `.upd` and their children), `src/updates.d/` (README and the waiting fragments' wording, into the new style, with targets and levels), `docs/specs/whats-new.md`, `docs/systems/whats-new.md`, the `kofi` and `noise` checks if the card's markup moves, a new check group for the card, a What's new fragment for this change, and this brief as `docs/briefs/whats-new-card.md`. Not `.claude/skills/` (a lessons tidy is running there), not `docs/lessons/` beyond this PR's own look back.
