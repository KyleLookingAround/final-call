# ADR-2026-09-27: Idea sessions ask where to focus before writing ideas

## Status

Accepted (the owner's instruction after the September idea board).

## Context

The September board (`docs/ideas/board-2026-09.md`) spread 60 ideas across every part of the game. The owner rated them all in under half an hour, but most (38) came back as a plain Like, so the board said little about where the owner wants the game to go. Ideas spread thinly over every area can't go deep in the one that matters.

## Options Considered

### Option 1: Ask about focus first, then write ideas for it
**Description:** An idea session opens with a short focus round: which parts of the game the owner wants to work on (gameplay, the region, the simulation, the look, anything), how much, and what feels flat today. Only then does it write ideas, weighted to the chosen areas.

**Pros:**
- Ideas go deep where the owner is looking, instead of wide.
- The owner's reasons are on record for later sessions.

**Cons:**
- Two rounds with the owner instead of one.

### Option 2: One board across every area
**Description:** As in September: a fixed spread of ideas, rated in one pass.

**Pros:**
- One round.

**Cons:**
- Mostly Likes, and little depth in the areas that matter.

## Decision

Option 1. Every idea session starts with a focus round, reasons from its answers which areas to write for, says that reasoning back to the owner, and only then writes ideas. Ideas for areas the owner left out are few or none. Both rounds can be one board, the ideas added once the focus is known.

## Consequences

- Idea briefs name a focus round as their first step.
- The focus answers are saved with the board's results in `docs/ideas/`.
