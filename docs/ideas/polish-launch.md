### Left out, with reasons

- **Labels under 11 px** (stat labels 10 px, chips 10.5 px, board pills 10 px, badges 8–9.5 px, phones): legible in practice and a deliberate compact style; not worth a batch.
- **Five NEW badges at once** (tab bar, level 3 and 9 saves): the saves come from the bot, which approves plans without opening tabs; a player clears each badge by visiting the tab.
- **The first earnings happen off-screen** (a car-park fee while the tour points at gate A1): too small to notice.
- **The board's flaps showing a wrong letter**: a screenshot caught the split-flap spin mid-way; it settles.
- **`ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`** in the console: this sandbox's proxy blocking Chromium's own background requests and Google Fonts, not the game.

### Checked and found fine

- **What a shared link shows:** title (41 characters) and description (137) in plain UK English; absolute `og:url` and `og:image`; `og:image` 1200×630 and matching `preview.jpg` (122 KB); `twitter:card` `summary_large_image`; the image reads at a 300 px thumbnail; the icon is clean at 180 px. `npm run preview` makes the same picture.
- **Speed:** 60 fps median everywhere (desktop and phone, level 9 and a new game, the Masterplan, region and world views); at most 1.2% of frames over 50 ms at 4× on level 9. First paint in 400–500 ms. No console errors from the game.
- **Desktop, tablet (both ways) and 1920×1080**, day and night, levels 5 and 9: consistent cards, buttons and fonts; the large-screen breakpoint scales the panel with the map; tablet portrait gets the bottom sheet, landscape two panes.
- **Phones:** no horizontal scroll; the camera band (off to large) never covers the toolbar, in either orientation; the bottom sheet snaps; full screen's Manage drawer works; the Masterplan, Help, What's new and level-up card match each other at 320 and 390; taps on tabs, chips, speed and full screen respond within 300 ms.
- **Guided start:** every step but step 2 on a phone advances on a real tap; the coach box never overlaps the spotlight.
- **Feel:** an on-time departure plays a till sound with an "ON TIME +$" floater, a late one a low tone with "LATE n MIN"; a level-up plays the fanfare; the first 30 minutes aren't dead (the first plane taxis in within seconds and a tip arrives before the first departure).
- **Menus that work:** Routes (Your routes, New routes), Sales (Prices, Shops, Landside), Region (Transport, Development, Overview) and Airfield's Projects (shown only once one is available) are clear, and locked things are hidden, not greyed.
- None of the last audit's fixed rows has come back, apart from row 8 (above).
