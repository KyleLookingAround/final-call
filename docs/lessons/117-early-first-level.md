# The first level-up in the first morning (#117) · 28 Sep 2026

- **Numbers:** estimate $12; about $8.40 at the PR's opening, with the six baseline runs, three rebuild runs, a three-seed experiment and a fresh review inside that. Started 04:46 UTC, PR open as a draft at 05:07 and ready at 05:08, well inside the 06:30 cut-off.
- **What found the lever fast:** an hour-by-hour trace of the bot's first two days (a 20-line script over `tools/bot.js` with `every:1`) showed in one run why level 1 took a day and a half: 600 passengers on one gate is 30 hours of flying, and the second gate (400) only comes once the tips' upgrades are bought. Both had to move, and the requirement is the only place they meet. Worth keeping as a habit for pacing questions: trace the bot at the hour before touching the baselines.
- **The experiment that said no:** bringing level 2 forward by asking for two gates instead of three (the brief said it "may" move) gave level 2 twenty hours earlier and everything after it later, `off` on every seed. Three 700-hour seeds took a minute in parallel and settled it with numbers in the decision record instead of an argument. A cheaper level can slow the game: the reward and points get spent before the thing the next level needs.
- **Friction:** the fresh reviewer ran `npm run build` and check groups in the same checkout while the full suite was running, and a `news` click timed out under the load (it passed alone straight after). Next time, tell a reviewer not to build or run checks in the working checkout, or run the suite after the review. Also, `tools/graph.mjs` only links a check to a system when the check file itself names the system's functions or constants: a check that plays through `tools/bot.js` is invisible to the graph unless it reads something like `S.LEVELS` directly. Now noted in the check.
- **Main moved three times while the PR was open** (#106, #108, #111, #113), and the Catch-up workflow merged each into the branch within a minute, twice rejecting a push of mine. Famous faces (#111) changed the dice from level 3 on, so the before and after runs had to be made again against the new `main` (six runs, four minutes in a worktree) and the tables, the decision record and the playbook rewritten; the Balance label went on twice. On `main` at #102, seed 3 didn't reach level 9 inside 1,150 hours; at #111 it does again. A night with this many merges wants the baseline runs made last, once, against the head that will merge, rather than first.
