# Phone and touch polish: #84 phone chrome, #87 overlay cards, #88 Masterplan on a small phone (scroll), #107 the top bar, #112 where players look, #124 the first minute · 27–28 Sep 2026

- **Numbers:** #84 estimate $8, $5.97 and 285k by the merge; a fresh review; all four batches pure CSS. #87 estimate $5, $6.18; opened 17:44, merged 18:16. #107 estimate $5, $2.89; opened 21:56. #112 estimate $12, about $5; stopped from about 22:20 to 04:05 by the account's limit; opened 04:19 and merged 04:34. #124 estimate $8, $42.53 and 714k of 1M by the merge (5.3×): opened 09:02, merged 09:59, seven merges of `main` (all but one Catch-up fast-forwards; the one real conflict was both branches adding a `FIELDS` line, resolved by adopting `main`'s new `ADDED` list in the `migrate` check). #88's merge-chasing is in `88-busy-main-before-catch-up.md`.
- **The habit that paid every time: measure, then fix.** #87 read `#coach`, `#goal`, `.stats` and `#tabs` `getBoundingClientRect()` at the brief's sizes and found the tab bar or stats row (not the goal bar) was what overlapped. #107 wrote `tools/checks/topbar.mjs` first and watched it fail on `main` at 320, 390 and 844×390, and a `git worktree` of the commit before #84 gave the "before" pictures in one build. #112 removed what sat above a moved section before taking the picture, to show each move on a phone. #124's early attempts didn't reproduce the reported layout bugs and cost most of the overrun in verification; the geometry needed three JS fixes (a hud-avoidance clamp, a `tourRect` return shape, a side-panel overlap clamp) that one measurement pass up front could plausibly have found together. → Take the measurement/screenshots pass before writing the fix (`feature` playbook, step 4).
- **Checks on the whole, not the part:** #84 grew the bar's buttons to 42 px with no check on the bar (#107: how many fit, not just how big each is). #112 built the Fleet tab before measuring whether an eighth fits the strip (59 px over on desktop, labels overrunning at 320 px), so that half moved to a later PR; `docs/SYSTEMS.md` "UI" now says how tabs share the strip. A fixed-width budget is tight (390 portrait fits eight 40 px buttons with 2 px spare); a new top-bar button moves the steps in `shell.html` and the check with it. Measure `#stage` before choosing breakpoints: the page gutter and the 40 px camera bands leave 353 px of map at 844×390 and 254 px at 667×375, and container queries on `.stage` handle full screen and landscape.
- **What the reviews caught:**
  - #84: `.recrow .btns .chip`'s own padding rule (three classes) beats the plain `.chip` touch rule (one class), so the "Preview" button stayed small; every other touch fix relied on equal-specificity selectors later in the stylesheet, which a chip variant with its own override escapes. Grep every use of a class before assuming one generic rule covers it.
  - #87: `tourStep`'s last-resort fallback and tall-target override still measured against the spotlighted rect, not the widened avoid-zone, so a short landscape viewport could land the coach box back on the stats row (a size not among the brief's four). → Review the whole function a fix touches. It also flagged the unified close button visibly changing the level-up card's own; that was the intent (one shared style), confirmed by screenshot.
  - #88 (scroll): `planUnlockLine` listed only aircraft, route and transport unlocks, so a recommended plan elsewhere showed less than an ordinary card; and scrolling the whole ready card into view (`block:'nearest'`) broke again after a touch-target batch grew `.chip` padding. → Scroll the smallest anchor that proves the section is reachable (`[data-sec="ready"]`), and only a check measuring pixels catches a regression.
  - #124: a runtime latch on the "try 4×" tip cleared only on its own button, so a player using the ordinary speed control would have it (and every later advisor tip) stuck forever; a local bot comparison against `main` (seed 1, 50 hours) also showed the same bug silently moving `PLAY`, since the bot reads `advise()` every step but never changes speed. → A UI latch the bot can't clear moves `PLAY`.
- **Also:** #124's brief default (#105's "option A: no pacing change") had been overtaken by the owner's decision before the PR closed; read the issue's current state and comments, not just the brief (`feature` playbook, step 1). #87 confirms that scoping a brief's "may touch" list tightly (its overlay classes and two game files, explicitly not `.chip`, `.hud` or the tab bar) is what keeps a PR out of the merge-chase.
