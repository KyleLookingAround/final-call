# Release audit batches: #164 P4 labels, #165 P3 small screens, #166 F1 tips, #170 terminal polish, #171 B1 pacing, #172 loose ends · 29 Sep 2026

The second wave of the release audit, each batch in its own files (`159-release-fix-batches.md` has the first wave).

- **Numbers:** #164 (P4) no cost read. #165 (P3) estimate $12, cost unknown (usage not available in the tools). #166 (F1) estimate $15, 03:18 to 04:01, one new check group (`tips`, 11 checks), bot seeds 1–3 before and after, one full local check and a rerun of the five groups it failed. #170 (terminal polish) estimate $18, twelve bot runs and forty screenshots, about 35 minutes. #171 (B1) estimate $15, 03:19 to 04:45, about 50 bot runs of 1,200 hours, one full local check, no helper agents. #172 (loose ends) well under its $6 estimate, one CI round. `get_session` showed no cost yet for most at the first stopping point.
- **Went well:**
  - **Side-by-side variants without a rebuild race** (#166, #171): copy `build/` and `tools/` (or the `main` build) into a folder per variant and start its three seeds there, three to nine at a time, with a two-line status script printing each run's `LVLAT`, `PLAY` and final fare.
  - **Measure before touching CSS or code:** a `getBoundingClientRect` script on the stage, hud and panel found #165's wrapped top bar (the map is 198 px wide at 568×320, not the bar's fault) in one run; tracing the first flight minute by minute found #171's stowing bottleneck (everyone at the gate half an hour early, the aisle seating under one a minute); one throwaway Playwright script (`build/shots-term.mjs`, never committed) took #170's every size, floor and zoom.
  - **`PLAY` identical is a fast proof** (#170, #171): it showed pure swaps such as `R.fx.x>G.clock` → `weather.on('x')` changed nothing, that row 18 left the default alone, and that row 20's first try did nothing at all.
  - #164's `money()` and Load each got a check first-hand (`rules`, `saves`); the Load check drives the real button with a 3.5 s wait.
- **Lessons:**
  - **An audit row's files and options can be wrong.** #164: the Crews, Routes › New and Layouts notes lived in files the brief kept for other batches, so only Fleet shipped (→ the `coordinator` playbook now plans a loose-ends batch at the end of each wave). #170: O5's two pay options were written before anyone looked at the wage line (all wages about 2% of income, about 90% of it crew wages that pay doesn't touch), so no multiplier could make pay a choice: measure the share of the thing a lever acts on before writing its options into a row (`balance` playbook). #171: `STAND` in `01-constants.js` is overwritten by each layout, so row 20's cheaper gate changed nothing; a comment on `STAND` now says where gate prices live, and the `balance` playbook says to run one seed and check `PLAY` moves before tuning a constant.
  - **The bot's fare is decided in the first morning** (#171): it raised fares under a rating of 70 and lowered them only at 92, and a new airport starts at 60, so a slow first morning left seed 1 at 240% for 1,000 hours, stuck at City Airport. The bot now stops raising at the advisor's tips (120%), and the `balance` playbook says to read the snapshots' `fare` when one seed lags. #166 and #170 both hit the same stall on `main` (seed 1 at level 3, hour 149.7) and reported it to B1 rather than fixing it: not a tip and not a pay lever.
  - **Run the full check once the game code is in** (#166): four groups outside the ones run (`market`, `daystats`, `rating-day`, `graph`) failed on purposeful changes, one an old check asserting the audit's bug and one a marginal pass mark (fog's pull, 1.04 against 1) that gate servicing moved to 0.83. One throwaway edit (servicing off, the same numbers as `main`) proved the cause. Guard checks that compare a seeded play with `main` (`rules` passenger states, `weather-fx`, `daystats`) fail on any change that moves the dice on purpose: re-record one group at a time with its `*_RECORD=1` variable (`npm run check -- a b` runs only group `a`). Both are in the `feature` playbook.
  - **A tip or recommendation that buys something needs a rule for what makes it stop** (#166): "replace with bigger planes" never went away, since buying one doesn't sell the old. The fresh review caught it. A scripted player that follows the game's own guidance (#166's `tips` group has one) is worth keeping in `tools/` for the next guidance change.
  - **Row 5 can't be proved by a group that doesn't reach the wall** (#166): the follower reached Regional in 200 hours, not City Airport, so it proved the fare cap but couldn't show the wall lifting.
  - **Measure a label's width, don't guess it** (#170): a condensed font's width was guessed and the page falls back to a wider face when Google Fonts is out of reach, so shop names ran into their counts. `ctx.measureText` fixed it.
  - **Speed is noisy on a busy machine** (#171): `scene` read 0.321–0.333× on `main` and 0.32–0.374× on the branch against a ±0.05 budget. Compare with `main` on the same machine (`balance` playbook).
  - **Read a pass count against the group sizes** (#171): `npm run check -- rules weather-fx daystats` printed 27/27, which was `rules` alone (`tools/check.mjs` reads one argument), and `weather-fx` looked flaky for minutes.
  - **Don't chain a `git stash` behind a slow run** (#164): the "without the fix" run of the Load check never happened. `git stash` is in the `feature` playbook.
  - **Handed-over work is closed by a loose-ends batch** (#172): the terminal pass handed `66-floors.js` over once it merged, so `flyTo` now clamps to `camBounds()` and the `camera` check (320×568, 390×844, 1440×900) covers flying to the foot of the map; a phone camera margin (`camPad`, `measureCam` in `13-camera.js`) shipped with it.
  - #165's choices, for whoever changes the top bar again: at panel widths under 300 px the stats drop "Last hour" and the rating's number, to keep cash whole; tab labels depend on tab count (`:has()`), so six tabs or fewer keep them at 390; #164's row 38: with no `viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-top)` is 0 in a normal tab, so the camera band defaults to none there.
