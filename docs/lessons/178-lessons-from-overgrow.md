# Lessons from Overgrow, carried into the playbooks (#178) · 29 Sep 2026

- **Numbers:** written in the same session as Overgrow's PR (its lesson has the figures); docs only, no bot run and no speed budget.
- **What Overgrow has that is only context here** (not a playbook line):
  - **Briefs answer "How it fits and grows".** Overgrow's brief template has twelve questions (where a mechanic is born, how it carries across the levels, its loops, who does it once the player stops, its lever, its map impact, its Explain card, its economy, carbon and land, polish, its lesson, how it unfolds), and `tools/brief.mjs` fails a brief that leaves them unanswered. Final Call's audit found the game stops asking something new after City Airport (`ideas/release-audit.md`); a brief that has to say what a feature adds at the levels after its own is one way to notice that early. Whether Final Call wants the section is the coordinator's and owner's call.
  - **A systems web.** Overgrow keeps one table per system across all its levels (`specs/overgrow/systems-web.md` in Overgrow) and briefs name its rows. Lesson from writing it: write one system's table in full first, agree its depth, then do the rest at that depth (`lessons/31-systems-web.md`); a column added by script from one table of values beats editing 28 tables by hand. Final Call's `systems-review.md` spec is the nearest thing; the web is heavier.
  - **A new brief rule needs a grandfather list.** When Overgrow's `brief` check gained a required section, briefs already in flight failed until their names were listed beside `BEFORE` in `tools/brief.mjs`, and each merge of `main` brought one more (`lessons/31-systems-web.md`). Useful only if Final Call adds a rule to its own `brief` check.
  - **Instruments unfold as the player gains influence.** Every system runs from the first hour and the map is whole; panels and levers appear at the first choice that touches them, from one table, one sign a batch (`decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-unfolding.md` in Overgrow). Final Call's release audit found three NEW badges and two new tabs arriving at once at level 1 (row 28); the ADR is a design answer to the same problem, for the owner to weigh, not something to copy.
  - **Pacing judged by the longest quiet stretch, not the level hours** (`decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-strategic-and-long.md` in Overgrow): a milestone later than its range needs no agreement while the player always has something to decide or change. Final Call's baselines are level hours; its audit's walls (level 3 to 4, level 8 to 9) were quiet stretches. This changes how the owner judges pacing, so it is in the PR description for the coordinator, not in the `balance` playbook.
- **Lessons:**
  - Final Call's playbooks already held most of Overgrow's process lessons, since Overgrow copied them on 28 Sep; the new lines are the ones Overgrow learnt after the copy (the coordinator's sweep by known ids, when to hand over, a pass mark's ceiling, waiting for state in browser checks, checking a reviewer's claim).
  - Two lessons the brief names (the playtest before calling a level done, a check-in about 30 minutes after starting a session) have no written source in Overgrow's repo, so neither is here.
