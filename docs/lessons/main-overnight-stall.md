# The overnight stall, 27–28 Sep 2026

- **Numbers:** ten default-model sessions started within 45 minutes (21:00 to 22:20 UTC), which spent the account's five-hour usage allowance in about 80 minutes; every one of their turns was killed mid-way, before any could book the `send_later` its brief told it to. The coordinator's own check-in, booked for 22:19 UTC, wasn't delivered until 03:56 UTC. The stall ran from 22:20 to 03:56, about five and a half of the twelve hours the sessions had to work.
- **Went well:** once the reset landed, the sessions that did have open PRs picked up where they left off; no work was lost, only time.
- **Lessons:**
  - Ten default-model sessions started close together spend the five-hour allowance in under an hour and a half, not the many hours a lighter run gets. → A cap of about four default-model sessions at once, the rest on the cheaper model, starts staggered, and nothing new started on `allowed_warning` (`coordinator` playbook §5, `feature` playbook item 2). This is the second sighting of lesson #18's same warning.
  - A session's turn killed by the usage limit loses its own one-shot check-in with it, so nothing wakes it after the reset until the next scheduled sweep — here, over five hours away. → An hourly Routine that starts a fresh session each time, so it's never queued behind a stopped one, and re-wakes anything the limit stopped (`coordinator` playbook §11, `docs/briefs/watchdog.md`).
  - Refactor 8 changed how weather is read while eight feature sessions had branches open; each needed its own merge commit through the new read, and #102 went red after a clean Catch up merge even though nothing in its own diff had changed. → A refactor that changes what other files call runs in a quiet window, never alongside a wave of feature sessions (`feature` playbook, splitting work; `coordinator` playbook §5).
  - CI takes about 8 minutes; sessions were polling every 20–30 and the coordinator every 30–45, so most of a PR's wall time was spent waiting for the next check-in rather than for CI itself (first seen with #22, #23 and #24 booking their own check-ins alongside the coordinator's, 26 Sep). → Sessions subscribe to their own PR's events and keep a `send_later` only as the fallback (`steward` playbook, check-ins; `docs/briefs/TEMPLATE.md`).
  - At least four look backs recorded the PR tool appending a "Generated by" footer that then failed the Description check, each fixed by hand after the fact. → The check now strips a trailing footer itself before failing anything (`.github/workflows/description.yml`).
  - Without a `CATCH_UP_TOKEN` secret, Catch up's own dispatched Checks run sits next to a `pull_request` run needing approval that cancels it, so a session has to dispatch Checks by hand after every merge to `main`. → Documented either way in the `steward` playbook; the owner is adding the secret on 28 Sep.
