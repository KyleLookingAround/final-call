# The airport scene

**The airport scene: drawing layers, the lighting pass and the real airport's parts** (`50-scene.js`, `51-markings.js` to `55-vehicles.js`, `draw()` in `12-drawing.js`; spec `docs/specs/real-airport.md`, version 31).
- **How the parts fit.** `draw()` is `sceneView` (the camera, the canvas cleared, and `V`) and the layers in order, nothing else (refactor 9 of `docs/specs/systems-review.md`; the `scene` check fails any canvas call outside a layer). Every file registers its own drawing, and within a layer entries run in file order. By layer: `airfield` has the ground and `drawAirfield` (12-drawing.js), with the runways' markings (`rwyMarks`, called from it); `apron` has the grid, the taxiway band and the layout's apron furniture (12), then the taxi lines, stand markings and masts (markings), puddles, settled snow and the windsock (weather); `stands` has each stand's paint and plane (`drawStandApron` calls `drawPlane`, 12) and then the vehicles; `bridges` has the air bridges (12); `lit` starts with `lightPass()` (50-scene.js), which tints by the hour (`grade`), darkens, and draws `LIGHTS`: the masts, runway and taxiway lights (markings) and each plane's lights (`PL_LIT`, gathered as the planes draw); then the halls' lit windows (markings) and the puddles' shine (weather); `terminal` has the halls and their furniture (`drawTerminal` and the parts' `TERM_DRAW`), the layout's tubes, tent and links, and the FIZZCO banners (42-terminal.js); `landside` has the forecourt, station and car park (`drawLandside`, 07-passengers.js); `pax` has the passengers (`drawPax`, 12); `roofs` has the roofs, only when the player has stepped up to the roof; `signs` has the gates' cards, placed clear of each other (`placeGateCards`, 12), and each queue's sign (42); `weather` has the pickets of a strike (28-region-weather.js), then rain, falling snow, fog and cloud shadows; `top` has the floaters (04-effects.js) and clears the frame's plane lights. `LAYERS`, `LAYER` and `LIGHTS` are defined in `01-constants.js` so that files before `50-scene.js` can register. What only changes with the layout or what's built is cached and rebuilt when its key changes (`mkKey` for markings, `roofNow` for roofs); everything else is drawn each frame, skipped off screen and thinned out when zoomed out.
- **Together.** With all five parts in (version 31), a frame of the worst scene draws in 0.45× of calibration on Classic desktop, 0.36× on Midfield desktop and 0.35× on Midfield phone with roofs drawn (medians of three `scene` runs; 0.34×, 0.32× and 0.32× before the parts), and 0.37×, 0.38× and 0.37× once the roof became a floor left off by default; all under the 0.55× budget, and single runs vary by about ±0.05×. Markings and lights cost the most; trim there first if it nears the budget.
- **Layers.** The airport view draws bottom to top in the layers named in `LAYERS`: `airfield`, `apron`, `stands`, `bridges`, `lit`, `terminal`, `landside`, `pax`, `roofs`, `signs`, `weather`, `top`. Something new on the map is a function added to its layer (`LAYER.apron.push(V=>…)`), not a line in `draw()`. Each gets `V`, the frame's view (reused, not copied): what's on screen in world units (`x0`, `x1`, `y0`, `y1`), the scale and zoom (`k`, `z`), seconds (`t`), the darkness (`d`, from `darkness()`), the hour and `derived()` (`D`). It draws in world coordinates and leaves the canvas as it found it. While Day in a minute plays (`R.dimT`), `sceneView` takes the hour and darkness from the playback (`dimFrame`, `docs/systems/day-in-a-minute.md`). The region and world views draw none of them.
- **Lighting pass.** `lightPass()` is the first entry of `lit`: by night it darkens the apron and the forecourt (the terminal stays lit inside), then draws every function in `LIGHTS` additively. A light is `lamp(x,y,r,rgb,a)`, a radial glow skipped when off screen (`inView`). The stands' floodlights are the first entry.
- **Passengers** (`drawPax`, 12-drawing.js, on the `pax` layer) are drawn in batches: one path per look (fill, outline and its width) for all the dots, with buggies drawn before them and bags, laptops and shuffle rings after. Thousands of passengers cost a few dozen fills. With the gate lounges' seats in one path per colour (`drawTerminal`), this took the worst scene from 0.371× to 0.324× of calibration (medians of six runs each against `main`, the terminal-place groundwork). Close up (zoom 0.6 and more) each dot is filled on its own, which is quicker there than one big path.
- **Gate cards** (`placeGateCards`, called from the `signs` layer, 12-drawing.js) never let two stands' cards cover each other or their own text. Each built stand's card starts at its natural spot (`badgeRect`, from `placeBadges`'s `BADGE[i]`); ordered nearest the camera first, each one is dropped, then raised, then dropped or raised twice as far, until it clears every card and tag already placed, and one that still can't clear shows only its stand number (`gateTag`), which also reserves its own small space so a later card can't cover it either. A handful of rectangle tests a frame, on the stands `signs` was already going to draw. The `phone-bugs` check plays a seeded busy hour at phone, tablet and desktop widths and fails on any overlap (#119).
- **Photo mode** (`62-photo-mode.js`, `docs/systems/photo-mode.md`) can draw another hour and sky: `sceneView` reads the hour through `drawnHour()` and `54-weather.js` reads `weather.on`/`until` through `drawnWx` (over `drawnFx()`), both the game's own while photo mode is off.
- **Drawing is not play.** Nothing drawn may change `G` or the game's side of `R` or call `rnd()`; the `scene` check plays the same seed with and without frames drawn and compares. Animation runs off `V.t`.
- **Planes** (`52-planes.js`: `drawPlane` for the stands, `miniPlane` for the runway and the maps). A soft shadow offset by `PL_SUN` (fixed in world units, turned into each stand's frame, fading at night), engines under the wings with the airline's colour (`F.liv`, else `livery()`) on their cowls and the fin, and red and green wingtip marks. Inlets and flap lines only from `V.k` 0.55 up. At night each plane adds its lights to this frame's list (`PL_LIT`), which one `LIGHTS` entry draws and the `top` layer clears: wingtips and tail always, the beacon flashing while it's towed in or pushed back (`inbound`, `closing`), and on the runway strobes too. On the airport view `miniPlane` adds engines, a shadow while on the ground (scale 0.9–1.05) and lights; the runway's airborne shadow (drawn at alpha under 0.3 with no colour) is drawn dark. The maps' small planes are unchanged. The cabin inside is drawn as before. Checked in `scene`: shadow, livery on fin and engines, lights at night only, canvas left as found.
- **Speed.** The `scene` check times a frame of the worst scene (fully built, all in view, at night in a storm with fog and snow) for Classic and Midfield on a desktop and Midfield on a phone, against the calibration run; the spec shares its headroom between the parts.
- **Apron markings and lighting** (`51-markings.js`). On the `apron` layer: taxi centre lines (the band under the runway and each layout's `taxi` lines in `LAY.decor`, painted over their old dashes) with blue edge lights, and for each built stand a lead-in up the plane's centreline that forks into a curve each way at the tail, a stop bar ahead of the nose, red safety lines open at the tail, a floodlight mast and the stand's number painted at the lead-in (upright, from a `toW` position; hidden under a parked plane). `drawAirfield` calls `rwyMarks` for each open runway: side stripes, centre line, threshold stripes, aiming points, touchdown zones and the numbers. The lines are `Path2D`s in world units, built by `mkBuild` only when `mkKey()` changes (layout, size, what's built, the second runway, the open halls); detail too small to see is skipped (`V.k`). From dusk (`V.d>0`): runway edge lights (green across the thresholds), blue taxiway lights and the masts' lamps in `LIGHTS`, stamped from a small cached glow rather than a gradient each; on the `lit` layer, warm light spilling from every open hall's walls (lit windows). `lightPass()` first lays the time of day's colour (`GRADE`, `grade(h)`: blue before dawn, warm at sunrise and sunset, then the blue hour, a little blue all night, nothing by day) over the airfield, apron and forecourt. The `markings` check covers built stands only on every layout, lights only at night and on open runways, the grade, and the canvas left as found.
- **Roofs** (`53-roofs.js`, in the `roofs` layer). Every room in `ROOMS` that `roomOn()` says is built, and the baggage hall, has a roof in its own shape with a parapet, skylights down its middle (sky by day, lit from inside at night) and plant along one side; Denver's tent peaks and the glass over CDG's open middle go on top. The roof is a floor, not a zoom: `R.floor` (runtime: `'roof'`, `'up'` or `'down'`, starting on `'up'`, departures) picks it, and the camera bar's first stops, **Roof**, **Departures** and **Arrivals** (Roof and Halls on a layout with one floor) (Dep and Arr on a canvas under 560 px wide, as the board shortens them; `renderCam`, 13-camera.js), set it through `setFloor`. `roofA()` is 1 only on the roof in the airport view; `floorNow()` is the floor of halls drawn (0 or 1) and `onFloor(fl)` says whether a room or passenger with that floor shows. The halls are never covered on their own at any zoom (the owner's choice on 27 Sep 2026, replacing the zoom fade of #51; `docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-27-roof-is-a-floor.md`). **Nothing inside a hall is drawn under the roof:** `drawTerminal` draws only the outline of rooms not built yet and the forecourt's kerb, the parts' `TERM_DRAW` and the layout's tubes and tent are skipped, and `drawPax` skips anyone under a roof (`underRoof`); a train link's two station marks are the one thing left under it. The FIZZCO banners move over the roof, on the `signs` layer. On a floor of halls, `drawRooms`, the halls' furniture (`TERM_DRAW` entries tagged with a floor) and `drawPax` show only that floor: Classic's departures upstairs and arrivals below ([Floors](floors.md)). A layout with one floor (`twoFloors()` false) shows everything on its one floor of halls, and its camera bar shows **Roof** and **Halls**. The `plans` check `scene: nothing is drawn inside a hall under the roof` spies on one frame of the worst scene on the roof. The plan (`roofNow`) is worked out again only when the layout or what's built changes; fine detail is dropped when zoomed out and rooms off screen are skipped. Taps go through to the shops and stands as before. The `roofs` check: the halls clear at every zoom, the Roof button up to the roof and down again, never over a room not built yet (and there once it is), and taps still reach shops and stands.
- **Vehicles on the apron** (`55-vehicles.js`, on the `stands` layer). `vehicleWork(i)` reads a stand's own state — nothing new. It's a turnaround when the stand's plane is docked (`F.plane.state`: `deplaning`, `turnaround`, `boarding` or `closing`) or easing back off the stand (`S.out`, 08-stands.js); anything else, or a remote stand, and there's nothing to draw. A turnaround gets a fuel truck and a catering truck parked on the apron side of the stand (away from the bridge) and, once the bridge or stairs are mostly out (`S.ext>0.6`) and bags are still moving (`F.hold<F.bagsIn` or `F.arr.unloaded<F.arr.bags`), a baggage tractor with carts running the hold's own cart path (`paths()`, 04-geometry.js — this is where the cart moved from `drawBridge`, 12-drawing.js). Easing back gets a tug hitched to the nose instead, the same shape the plane's own arrival tow uses. The `vehicles` check proves it's never drawn at an empty stand or one still on its way in.
- **Weather you can see** (`54-weather.js`; check `weather`). Rain streaks, puddles, settled snow, fog banks, cloud shadows and a windsock, reading `weather.on` ([Weather and events](weather.md); through `drawnWx`, so photo mode can pick the sky) and `wxForecast()` and never changing them. Rain and falling snow moved here from `draw()` (`12-drawing.js`), unchanged apart from running off `V.t` instead of `performance.now()`. `wetness()` and `snowCover()` read straight off `weather.until('rain')`/`weather.until('snow')` (a rolling "until" game-clock, nudged on while the weather's active) and fade to 0 over the minutes after, with no saved field of their own: puddles (`apron`, shining in `lit` at night) and settled snow (`apron`, one wash clipped out over every built stand so ground crews keep them clear) follow that fade; fog calls `drawFog` (`29-region-map.js`) through `drawnFog()` (`62-photo-mode.js`). The windsock (`apron`) is always up; cloud shadows (`weather`) drift over a little darker and faster as `wxForecast()` sees a storm closing in.
