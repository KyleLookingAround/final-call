# Day in a minute

**Day in a minute** (`63-day-in-a-minute.js`; spec `docs/specs/day-in-a-minute.md`): a time-lapse of yesterday, played back over the airport view in about a minute.
- **Recording.** `dayRec`, a `MINUTE` hook every 5 game minutes (last in `CLOCK_ORDER.MINUTE`), packs what's on the airport into a sample (`dimSample`): each stand's plane (its `AIRCRAFT` index, airline colour and whether it's arriving, docked or leaving), the runway's queues and the movement under way, the check-in, security and passport queues, the passengers outside the cabins counted in a 60-unit grid (the heat), and the day so far from `G.dstat`. All in typed arrays.
- **The ring.** `R.dim` (runtime, never saved) holds today (`cur`) and yesterday (`prev`, with the day report from `G.lastDay`); at the day change today becomes yesterday and the day before goes. At most `DIM_CAP` (300) samples a day, about 215 KB for a full late-game day. A clock that goes backwards or jumps more than an hour between samples (a new game, another save, a headless stretch) starts it again; it starts afresh after a reload.
- **Read only.** It returns at once with `R.sim`, writes nothing to `G` and never calls `rnd()`.
- **Playback.** `dimBtn()` puts **Yesterday in a minute** under the day report in Office › Money once an hour or more of yesterday was recorded (`dimReady`). `dimPlay` pauses the game (`setSpeed(0)`), goes to the airport view, zooms out (`focus('all')`) and shows the bar (`#dim` in `src/shell.html`); the stage's other controls hide (`.stage.dimming`). `sceneView` calls `dimFrame` while `R.dimT` is set: it works out the replayed clock from the time since the start (a whole day in `DIM_SECS`, 60 s), and sets `V.hour` and `V.d` so the grade, the night and the lights follow it. The layers then wash over what's live and draw the recording: the runway (`airfield`), each built stand with its recorded plane easing on and off (`bridges`, before the lighting pass so it's lit like the rest), the halls' heat and queue tints (`pax`), and the stands' live cards covered (`signs`). In full screen the panel slides away (`drawer(false)`). A tap anywhere (the overlay, a tab, the panel), Escape or Space stops it (`dimStop`), and so does the end of the day; the airport's camera, the view and the speed go back. Other letter keys wait while it plays; typing in the panel and the browser's own keys are left alone.
- **Checks.** `day-in-a-minute`: after a seeded day the recording holds the whole of yesterday within its caps and 2 MB; nothing is recorded headless; recording costs at most 0.01× of the `perf` budget; the button plays it and every frame draws on a desktop and a phone, and after a change of layout; a tap stops it and the end stops it; `G` and the random stream match a run without it. `clocks` has `dayRec` in its recording.
