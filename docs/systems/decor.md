# Decor and local names

**Decor and local character** (`69-decor.js`; spec `docs/specs/terminal-place.md`, step 4 of its order of work; check group `decor`). Planters, benches, art and a grand departures board in the terminal's halls, and shops, a café and murals named after the region's places. Drawing only: the player places nothing, nothing is saved, the rating never sees it, it draws no `rnd()`, and nothing reachable from `update()` calls it, so `PLAY` and `STATE` are unchanged.
- **Where it goes** (`decor()` → `[{hall, kind, x0, y0, x1, y1, fl, …}]`). Worked out from `LAY.term`'s halls and `ROOMS` for the layout as built, so it follows every layout and any later floor plan: each hall that exists (`roomOn`, not `open`, not the terrace) and is at least 30 units across (not the customs corridor or the hotel walkway). Along the walls, at points spread round each hall by the golden ratio, it fits a board first in the check-in hall, the market place and the arrivals hall, then planters, benches and murals in turn, up to 24 a hall. A place is taken only if it keeps clear (`decObst`) of every counter, desk, lane, booth, e-gate, carousel (all eight, whichever a flight uses), queue slot, meeting spot and doorway or floor link (the same radii as the check, plus 3), of the straight walk between any two doorways of a hall and from them to the desks people walk to (11 units either side), of the market place's furniture (`mktPlan`), the halls' own furniture and labels, and of decor already placed on its floor. Decor on two floors may share the same ground, but the obstacles don't know floors: a queue downstairs keeps the gallery above it clear too, as `decor: never in the way` measures. Most of Classic's upper halls are one wide doorway from end to end, so their decor stands along the side walls.
- **With the building and the level.** Each hall's list is worked out once per layout and what's built; the level shows a share of it, the first 30% at level 1 up to all of it at level 9 (`decShare`), so a hall never loses decor as the airport grows, and a hall's decor comes when it's built (the hotel's with the hotel). Worked out again only when `ROOMS`, what's built or the level changes.
- **The roof terrace** (`decTerrace`). `67-terrace.js` already draws its rail, benches, parasols (dry, by day) and the telescope; decor adds planters at both ends of the passengers' side and on the public side, two benches on the public side clear of the spotters' places, and the café's name on an awning over its kiosk. Drawn on the Roof stop after the terrace (`LAYER.roofs`).
- **Local names** (`localNames()` → `[{text, place, hall, w}]`). From `PLACES` (not the airport or Lowmere), picked with a hash of the unit's index (`decHash`), never `rnd()`, so a save always shows the same names. Some coffee carts, cafés, bars, restaurants and newsagents (two in three, by the unit's hash) take a place's name and colour ("Eastmoor Café", "Millbrook Tap", "Castleton Inn"), a different place for each unit while there are enough, and never a name over 13 characters, so the level and the count still fit on the unit's front (the short endings let most places fit: a café, a deli or a newsagent takes Ashby, Eastmoor or Fellside, a bar or an inn any place but Harbourgate and Riverside Docks): `shopLook(j, type)` gives `drawShopUnit` (`46-market.js`) the look it draws with, and the panel keeps the type's name. Each mural is a place's ("Millbrook mural"), in that place's colour (`DEC_COL`). The terrace café is named for a place too. `w` is the text's width in world units, and the `decor` checks keep every sign narrower than its hall.
- **Drawing.** One `TERM_DRAW` entry (`drawDecor`), after the halls' furniture and before the passengers, so it's never drawn under the roof, and only on the floor shown (`onFloor`). Zoomed out (below 0.35) each item is one flat rectangle; closer, pots and leaves, bench slats, the board's lit rows and the murals' colours; a mural's caption (its place's name) only from 2.2×, where it's legible.
- **Speed.** No simulation. Drawing culls to the view and costs a rectangle or two for each item on screen (about 65 in a fully built Classic at level 9).
