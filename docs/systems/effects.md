# Effects: the rating and money ledger

**Effects: the rating and money ledger** (`04-effects.js`; the plan is step 3 of `docs/specs/systems-review.md`).
- **One entry point.** Every change to the rating or to cash goes through `effect(kind, cause, amount, at)`: `kind` is `'rep'`, `'earn'` or `'spend'`; `cause` is why the rating moved (a `REPWHY` key) or the money's kind (a `G.revBy` key); `at` is the map place where it happened when the caller knows it, a stand index on the airport or a line or place id in the region, else left out. `repAdj(d, why, at)`, `earn(v, kind, x, y, col, F, at)` and `spend(v, kind, at)` are its callers' names for it and keep their old arguments: `earn` still draws the `+$` floater at (x, y) and adds to the flight `F`'s takings.
- **What it keeps.** The rating's boosts (the safety plan and green developments raise gains), its 5–100 clamp, and `R.repWhy` (each cause's real change, summed); `R.repEv`, the last three hours of rating events as `[clock, cause, real, asked, at]`, which `repRecent()` totals for the Money tab and the advisor; money into `G.cash`, `G.earned`, `G.revBy`, the hour's bucket, `R.minEarn` and the day's stats; and `R.cashAt`, the last place each kind of money moved at. Nothing is allocated per call beyond the rating event, which was there before.
- **Causes.** `REPWHY[cause]` is `[what the advisor says it came from, upgrades that help, a fix to suggest]`, and `REPLBL[cause]` its line in the Money tab's rating list; a part adds its own (baggage adds `bags`, late runners `runner`). A new cause needs both, and the `effects` check plays a day to find any cause without them. The checks read the tables as `__sim.R.effects`, since this file loads before `SIMX`.
- **Places known today.** Stands for departures (`settle`: on time, late, queues, noise, cargo, handling), boarding (missed connections, priority boarding, buses to remote stands), seating (fares, help for passengers who need it), arrivals clearing, shops, crowded gate lounges, bags left behind, runners the gate closed on and passengers stranded overnight; the line for a line stopped by a fault; the event's place for event crowds. Region-wide causes (traffic, crowding), the layout's daily bonus, advertising and money with no floater carry none yet.
- **A rating that reflects the last day** (`docs/ideas/game-logic.md`, idea 1; #96; `docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-27-rating-last-day.md`). On for players. `R.rateDay` holds its constants (`RATE_DAY`). `window.__rateDay` is never saved: it can pass others to try, or `false` to play the old running sum (the bot's `--rate-day`, and the `rating-day` check).
  - `effect('rep', …)` adds each change (after the boosts) to its hour's bucket of a rolling 24-hour score (`rdScore()`, `{h, s, n}`), not onto `G.rep`, and to `R.rdSum`, the running total the `effects` check adds the causes up against. `punctual` and `late` count the departures, since every departure settles as one of the two. `R.repWhy` and `R.repEv` record the change as asked.
  - On the hour, after advertising, `rateDayEase` moves the rating 15% of the way towards `rateDayTarget()`: `68 + 32 × (the day's net score per departure ÷ 7)`, in the 5–100 clamp. Until 6 departures have gone in the last day, the target leans towards the rating as it is.
  - The score is runtime only. It starts again when a save loads (`G` is a new object), so a loaded airport holds its rating for an hour or two while the score fills. That's why it needs no saved field.
  - The rating chip (`repChipDay`, `15-panel.js`) adds an arrow for where the rating is heading, with the last three hours' biggest cause that way as its title. Tapping the rating opens Office › Progress, whose "Rating" list gives the causes.
  - What a departure scores: about +3.5 on time with short queues, down to −8 late. A well-run airport nets 4–5 a departure late in the game, so it sits at 85–90. Fog, delays and queues pull it down within hours.
