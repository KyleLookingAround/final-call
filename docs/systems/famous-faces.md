# Famous faces

**Famous faces** (`64-famous-faces.js`; spec `docs/specs/famous-faces.md`). Now and then an invented celebrity flies from the airport, from level 3 (`G.level>=2`).
- **Booking.** A `TERM_DAY` hook books a visit for tomorrow once `G.famous.next` comes round: `famousBook(d)` picks a kind (footballer, pop star, film actor, royal), an invented name from `FAMOUS_FIRST`/`FAMOUS_LAST` (or a title and realm from `FAMOUS_ROYAL`/`FAMOUS_REALM`) and an hour between 08:00 and 18:45, all through `rnd()`, and posts a Region news line. The next booking comes `famousGap()` days later: 6–9 at levels 3–4, 4–7 at 5–6, 3–5 from level 7. The first comes 1–3 days after reaching level 3.
- **Saved state.** `G.famous` (`FIELDS`, `03-state.js`) is `{next, v}`: `v` is the visit `{d, t, who, kind, st}` (`st` 0 booked, 1 on a flight, 2 over), with `flt`, `at` (the flight's stand) and `paid` (the busy hour's takings) once known. Older saves get `{next:0, v:null}`.
- **On the day.** A `TERM_MINUTE` hook (`famousMinute`), from the visit's hour, puts them on the earliest passenger flight at a stand leaving 40–300 minutes later and not boarding yet (a celebrity already aboard would never be seen), preferring one whose passengers are still arriving (`famousFlight`), and picks one of its passengers (`famousPax`, business class first). `R.famous` holds the flight, the passenger, the crowd's places and the busy hour; after a load or a new game it's rebuilt, on the same flight if it's still at its stand. No flight by the end of the day and the visit lapses.
- **The crowd.** Eight photographers on the pavement either side of the check-in doors (`DOOR`) and fourteen fans along the check-in side of the security barrier (`famousCrowd`): the photographers go 10 minutes after the celebrity is through security, the fans 30. A few fans stand outside the busy shop. Drawn from `TERM_DRAW` with a gold ring and ★ over the celebrity; flashes and waving are cosmetic and still with reduced motion.
- **On the roof.** `FAMOUS_MIN` hooks run each minute of the visit with `R.famous`: with the roof terrace built and open, a celebrity waiting in the market place with 40 minutes or more before their gate call goes up for 10–20 minutes, once, and the public side gains 6 photographers and 10 fans while they're there ([The roof terrace](terrace.md)). The crowd at check-in and security is unchanged.
- **The busy hour.** The shop the celebrity walks into, else the best café-type unit (`FAMOUS_CAFE`, `famousCafe`) once they head for the gate, takes 60% more on what it earns for an hour: each minute the rise in its `earned` is topped up through `earn(…,'shops',…,stand)`.
- **The rating.** When their flight leaves its stand, `G.history` says whether it was late: `repAdj(±1,'famous',stand)` and a news line. `REPWHY.famous` and `REPLBL.famous` ("Famous passengers") are added from this file.
- **The board.** `famousLine` fills `#ffLine` under the departures board from the day before until they've flown (`★ TOMORROW ~14:00 · …`, then the flight number); `famousStar(F)` puts a ★ before the city on their flight's row (`14-board.js`).
- **Checks.** `famous-faces` plays the level 5 save from a booking to the departure: the news and board line a day ahead, the crowd appearing and clearing, the busy hour and rating through the ledger with the stand as the place, no errors with `R.sim`, and the default for an older save.
