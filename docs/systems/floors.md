# Floors

**Halls on two floors** (`66-floors.js`, Classic's `term` table in `42-terminal.js`; spec `docs/specs/terminal-place.md`, "Two floors"). Classic's halls stand on two floors: departures upstairs (`fl` 1: check-in, security, the market place, and the gate lounges on the concourse), arrivals below (`fl` 0: immigration, reclaim, customs, the arrivals hall, the hotel walkway and the baggage hall). The concourse, the forecourt and the hotel have no floor and show on both. The other layouts keep one floor (`flatTerm()`, Classic's plan before it had two) until the floor plans part gives each its own.
- **The plan.** Every hall stayed where it was, so walks barely changed. Arriving passengers leave the concourse by their old door onto a gallery over immigration (`acr`, "TO ARRIVALS", `fl` 1) and ride down. The table's `plain` areas are floor that isn't a hall (upstairs over the arrivals halls, and the baggage hall under check-in downstairs) and `void` is the opening over reclaim; both are drawn only (on the `terminal` layer before the halls), never walked.
- **Floor links** are doorways between floors, `[hall, hall, x, y, half-width, 'esc'|'lift'|'stairs', people a minute]` (`isFloorLink`). Classic has an escalator (40 a minute) and a lift (8 a minute) from `acr` down to `imm`. `buildRooms` gives a link's way the step `10 + its index in ROOM_DOORS` and a cost of `LINK_C`; `route` asks `pickLink(p, j)` which of the links between the same two halls to take: the lift for `p.famL`, families and those who need help (arriving ones are marked `famL` as they take it), unless they're running late (`p.late` or running, `61-late-runners.js`); everyone else the nearest escalator or stairs. Late runners never get the lift.
- **Queuing and riding** (`rideLink`, called by `walk` for a link step): a passenger walks towards the link's entry, joins its queue (`R.flq`, runtime, per link and way, made afresh for each layout and load) once they reach its tail, goes one at a time at its rate, rides from entry to exit (`LINK_RIDE` minutes over `2 × LINK_RL` units; downwards riders go on at the left end) and changes room, and so floor, as they step off, standing there `LINK_OFF` minutes. `p.rideAt` holds where they are: `null` walking up, `-1` queuing, `-2` going on, else when they got on; `route` clears it. A queue's places are rows across the link's width going back from its entry (`linkSlot`); runners join it ahead of anyone not running (the roof terrace's stairs). Bags never use floor links.
- **Drawing.** `onFloor(fl)` (`53-roofs.js`) is true for a room or entry without a floor, on the floor shown, or on a layout with one floor (`twoFloors()`). `drawRooms` fills and walls only the rooms on the floor shown, and with two floors cuts a wall only for its own doorways. `TERM_DRAW` entries tagged with `onFl(fl, f)` draw only on their floor (departures and security 1, arrivals and baggage 0; the market place's floor 1 but its shop units on both; the famous faces' fans 1). The escalators and lift are drawn on both floors (a link is drawn only where one of its halls shows, so the roof terrace's stairs and lift show upstairs only), with the names of the halls above or below (`CUSTOMS ↓`) drawn dim on plain floor over them. `drawPax` hides passengers whose room is on the other floor, and `paxEase`'s catch-up follows the link's exit through `p.doors`, so nobody is drawn crossing a floor.
- **Going to a hall.** `hallLabel(id)` is where a hall's name is drawn (its top left corner, or its `lab`), as big as `labelSize` makes it, and its tap area grows with it. Tapping it (a `PAX_TAP` entry; halls with a sign keep their tap for the Terminal tab), or the dim name over it, a tip with `{hall: id}` or a board line calls `flyHall`/`flyTo`: the camera flies there (at least 1.1× zoom) and `setFloor` shows that hall's floor. An arrivals board line whose bags are on a carousel goes down to it; any other line goes up to its gate.
- **The chip.** Classic shows **Roof / Departures / Arrivals**; a layout with one floor shows **Roof / Halls** (`renderCam`, 13-camera.js), and `setFloor('down')` there shows the halls.
- **Old saves.** Passengers aren't saved (`resetAll` starts `R.pax` afresh), so every passenger in a loaded game starts out on their hall's floor; the `floors` check loads every save.
- **Checks** (`tools/checks/floors.mjs`, one three-hour play): the halls' floors and no overlap on a floor; floor changes only at a link (at least 50); families by lift (80% or more) and others not (5% or fewer); runners never by lift; nobody stuck; nobody drawn through a floor; the walks within 15% of `main` before two floors; taps and tips going to a hall's floor; old saves. `plans` holds `scene: one floor at a time`.
