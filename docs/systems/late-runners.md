# Late runners and passengers' stories

**Late runners and passengers' stories** (`61-late-runners.js`, spec `docs/specs/late-runners.md`, checks `tools/checks/late-runners.mjs`).
- **Final call** is `RUN_AT` (12) minutes before departure while the plane boards, the moment shops send out their last browsers (`46-market.js`, `p.late`) and security stops searching bags (`hurry`, `43-departures.js`).
- **Dawdlers.** When a gate is called, each of its passengers in a shop browses on with odds `DAWDLE` (0.15, `rnd()`), marked `p.late` so the shop keeps them until final call. This is the one look over all passengers, once per gate call, from the `TERM_MINUTE` hook.
- **Runners.** `PAX_STEP.toGate` is wrapped: from final call, anyone of that flight walking to the gate runs at `RUN_MUL` (1.8) times their pace, or `RUN_PRM` (1.2) for passengers who need help, never above `RUN_TOP` (300 px a minute) unless they were already faster, which keeps them under the `movement` check's top speed. The drawn position eases as for everyone (`paxEase`, `12-drawing.js`). Each flight's runners are in `RUNS` (a `WeakMap` from the flight: `list`, `n` who can miss, `holdAt`), so no flight or passenger gains a field.
- **The gate.** `BOARD_STATUS` (`14-board.js`) shows GATE CLOSING from `RUN_CLOSING` (5) minutes before departure while any runner is on the way, after TECH and CREW DELAY and before DELAYED, so FINAL CALL is still seen and announced. The board's flaps show a status too long for them a word at a time (`flapPage`). The gate badge draws it red (`statusCol`).
- **Holding and closing.** A runner who started before the departure time can miss. Once the departure time has passed and everyone else is seated, the gate holds `RUN_HOLD` minutes (3 for the Late passengers policy "Wait for them", 1 for "Close on time"), then closes: each runner still on the way leaves the flight (`F.booked` drops), costs `RUN_MISS` (0.6) rating as the cause `runner` at the stand, and walks slowly back to the market place (state `missed`) and out. Their fare was never taken; bags are left as they are. Runners who reach the airside after the departure time are waited for, as before.
- **Stories.** `TALES` (a `WeakMap` from the passenger) keeps a small record: how they came (`howCame`, from where `walkIn` or the hotel put them), and events noted as they leave a walking step (`onLeave` on `walkIn`, `bpGate`, `bpTap`, `repack`, `secOut`, `toShop`, `toMkt`, and the wrapped `toGate`), each with a mood change. Waiting steps (queues, shops, the market place, the gate) are never wrapped. Names, home towns and reasons for travelling come from the passenger's own `rand`, so they use no `rnd()`.
- **The card.** `PAX_TAP` (`13-camera.js`) lets a tap on the airport view pick the nearest departing passenger in reach (`openStory`); any other tap closes it, as does leaving the airport view. The card (`#paxcard`, CSS in `src/shell.html`) shows a name, their flight, up to eight timed lines (`taleLines`), a mood word and a few sentences (`taleText`); it refreshes every 400 ms, and an amber ring marks the passenger (`LAYER.pax`). Boarding is noted when the card sees it.
- Nothing is saved: a load starts runners and stories afresh.
