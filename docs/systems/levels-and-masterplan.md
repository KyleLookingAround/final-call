# Levels and Masterplan

**Levels and Masterplan** (`02-masterplan.js`, `09-construction-levels-days.js`, `18-masterplan-ui.js`). Level-ups and some goals give plan points. Consultants sell points from level 4. `GOALS` is a sequential list, and `curGoal()` returns the first goal that is unfinished and available. `recommendedTech(branch)` marks the lowest-tier plan ready to approve in each category as the one to pick next; it's display only, worded like the level-up card's "what this unlocks" line (`planUnlockLine`), and changes no cost, effect or unlock.

`LEVELS` (`01-constants.js`) holds each level's requirements (`req`: passengers flown, passengers in the last 24 hours from level 2, rating and gates open) and its cash reward; `checkLevel` (`09-construction-levels-days.js`) grants the next level every minute they're all met, and `levelChecks` lists them for the Office, each row also carrying a display size and a short unit word. Level 1 asks for 80 passengers flown, a rating of 50 and the gate you start with, so a new airport reaches it in its first morning (about game hour 4–5 with the bot's default play; `docs/specs/early-first-level.md`, `docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-early-first-level.md`); it pays $1,000 (#147, up from $500, so gate 3 comes sooner), level 2 asks for 1,600 passengers flown and level 3 for 8,000 (from 2,000 and 6,000: Regional Airport about 60 game hours in, City Airport where it was), and levels 7 and 9 ask 26,000 and 34,000 a day, within reach of eight gates. The `first-level` check keeps that first morning, and that at least one of the first three departures leaves on time on each of seeds 1–12 (#147: stowing a bag takes 2.3 minutes before Bigger overhead bins, down from 2.6, since a slow first morning also left the bot's fares high and one seed at City Airport for good), and the `rules` check keeps each level asking at least as much as the one before.

A curfew (Office › Policies › Night flights) closes the airport 23:30–05:30; `levelChecks` scales the next level's daily-passenger target to the 18 hours it's open while the policy is on, so a curfewed airport can still level up rather than being locked below the level that needs more than a curfewed day can ever carry. `PLAY` is unaffected with the curfew off (its default).

A level goal (`l1`–`l9` in `GOALS`) has no fixed text: `get t()` calls `levelGoalReq(n)`, which picks whichever of `levelChecks(n)`'s rows has the lowest value-to-target ratio — the requirement actually holding the level back — so the goal bar reads e.g. "International Airport: 5,800 / 8,000 a day" rather than counting levels reached. Its `p()` (`levelGoalProgress`) reads as met once `G.level>=n`, even if a requirement (the rating, an old save's numbers) has since slipped back below its target: reaching the level is the goal, not holding every number forever after, and old saves migrating `G.gdone` (`22-save.js`) depend on that. `low1` (keep 55% of shared travellers from Lowmere) sits after `l5` in `GOALS`, so it only becomes the current goal from Gateway Airport on, where slot agreements (`n_slots`) are within reach; before that a player had no lever to act on it. `checkLevel` marks a tab NEW only when a level-up actually reveals it (comparing `tabOpen` before and after the level changes), not every tab that happens to already be open.

`has(key)` answers whether a Masterplan-gated feature is unlocked. A key some plan's `u` list unlocks follows research (`G.tech`); a key with no such entry falls to `keyExists`, which checks the item is real in its own category (`UPG`, `AIRCRAFT`, `METHODS`, `SHOPS`, `MODES`, `DEV`, `STN_UP`, `LAYOUTS`, route tiers, `FEAT_NAMES`) — covering the game's many unlocked-from-the-start items (the starter plane, the base upgrades, bus lines) — and otherwise returns false, so a typo in a `has('…')` call hides its feature rather than showing it early. The `rules` check scans every literal `has('…')` call site in `src/game/` and fails if its key isn't real.
