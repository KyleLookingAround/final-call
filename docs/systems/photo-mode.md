# Photo mode

**Photo mode** (`62-photo-mode.js`; spec `docs/specs/photo-mode.md`; check `photo-mode`).
- **In and out.** The camera button (`#photob`) in the view controls calls `photoOn()`; on a phone (up to 600 px wide) the top bar keeps its room (the owner's call on 27 Sep 2026), as it does wherever the map is under 356 px wide on a touch screen (a phone on its side; `docs/SYSTEMS.md`, top bar), and **Photo mode** in How to play (`#photoHelp`) does it instead, closing the card first: `body.photo` hides the board, the side panel or sheet, the speed bar, the camera chips, toasts, the tip, the paused tag, the full-screen bar, the camera band and the guided start's spotlight, and fixes the stage over the whole screen (the CSS is at the end of `src/shell.html`'s styles). The photo bar (`#photobar`) sits at the foot, clear of the home bar. `photoOff()` leaves: **Done**, Esc (after any open card has had it), or a tap on the map, caught before the map's own tap so it picks no stand or tab, and the click a touch sends after it is eaten, since the panels are back under the finger by then; a drag or pinch still frames the view. Both call `resize()` at once, so the canvas matches the screen straight away. The level-up card, help and What's new still show over it.
- **Drawn only.** `R.photo` (runtime, `null` while off) holds the picked time (`PH_TIME`: Now, Dawn 06:36, Noon, Dusk 19:36, Night 23:00) and sky (`PH_SKY`: Now, Clear, Rain, Fog, Snow). Drawing reads them through `drawnHour()` (`darkness()` in `12-drawing.js` and `sceneView` in `50-scene.js`, so the grade, the darkness and every light follow it in the airport view, and the night and lit windows in the Region) and `drawnFx()` and `drawnWx` (its `on`/`until`, standing in for `weather.on`/`weather.until` in `54-weather.js` and the runways' snow in `drawAirfield`: a picked sky is just that weather, on for good, so puddles, settled snow and the windsock follow). `drawnFog()` stands in for `drawFog`'s own gate on `weather.on('fog')`: it sets the fog through `weather.set` for that one call and puts it back straight after. `G.clock`, `R.fx` and the random stream never change, and leaving clears it all.
- **The Region** has no weather layer of its own, so while photo mode is on, `photoTick()` (its own animation frame, queued after the game's, one loop per entry) lays the picked rain, snow or fog over the whole screen after `draw()` (`photoRegionSky`). The region's moving weather cells (`drawWeatherCells`, `G.wx`) are part of the map and stay as they are, Clear included: hiding them would mean editing `29-region-map.js`. On the world map the bar offers only Pause, the shutter and Done. Nothing runs while photo mode is off.
- **Pause** is the game's own (`setSpeed`); leaving puts the speed back as it was on entering.
- **The shutter** (`photoSave`) flashes the stage and makes `photoShot()`: the canvas at its full resolution with a small FINAL CALL tag in the bottom-right corner, as a PNG made synchronously so the phone's share sheet still counts it as the tap's. On a touch screen that can share files it opens the share sheet; otherwise, or if sharing fails, it downloads `final-call-<airline>-day-<n>.png`.
- **The check** (`photo-mode`) enters and leaves (through How to play on a phone, where the top bar must not have the button) at 320 px, phone, landscape phone, tablet and desktop sizes; draws every time with every sky in both views; shows the drawn hour darkens the frame (lamps aside); checks the PNG's size against the canvas; and compares `G`, `R.fx` and the random stream before and after.
