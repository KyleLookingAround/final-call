# Region

**Region** (`24-region-places.js` to `30-region-ui.js`). Bus, tram, rail, metro and high-speed lines, development sites, events, and weather.
- **Transport tab.** The summary's riders are the sum of every line's; it names the three busiest. A line in service with no riders says when a quicker line with riders stops at two or more of its stations and takes them.
- **How the map looks** (`29-region-map.js`, check `region-looks`). A dark transit-map style in the airport view's palette. The land, sea and towns (`paintLand`: gentle relief, shaded hills, hedged fields round the towns, woods, sand, shallows and depth lines, a river widening to the sea, each town a built-up area growing with its people, its streets and buildings) are painted once to an offscreen canvas (`regionTerrain`, cached in `RTER`) and stamped each frame without smoothing. The soft ground under it (colour, relief and hills, all gradients) is painted once a season at 0.4 scale (`paintGround`, `RGND`) and drawn into it. It's painted again only when its key changes: the scale band (the whole-region view's own scale, so that view stamps pixel for pixel, else 0.5 to 2, 1.5 at most on phones), the season, and how many buildings each town and station shows (which changes at most once a game day). While the zoom is moving it keeps stamping the old one, and paints once the zoom has been still for 250 ms (30 ms zoomed out, 75 ms zoomed in on a desktop). Its randomness is `seeded()`, never `rnd()`. The motorways, roads, traffic, sites and everything on the network are drawn each frame over it. Lines get one dark casing under them, so lines side by side read as one band; served stations a soft shadow. The airport is its own shape: grass inside the fence, runways with piano keys and centre lines, taxiways, the apron, terminal, piers and car park.
- **Labels** (`rLabel`, `flushLabels`). Place names, populations, station names, line codes, site names and the airport's name are queued through the frame and placed after the stations, highest priority first: each tries its spot and then its alternatives (above, below, beside), and is left out if it would overlap one already placed, sit under the speed or camera buttons (measured once a second), or, for minor ones, cover a station. A label is drawn only when its place is on screen, and is nudged to stay inside the view. Names grow a little as you zoom in, and have a dark halo. The screen boxes placed are kept in `R.regLbl` for the check. Alerts (events, track full, building, weather, gridlock, roadworks) are drawn straight over them, as before.
- **Night on the map.** `drawRegion` lays the time of day's colour (`grade`, shared with the airport view) and the night's darkness as one wash after the land and sites and before the network, so lines, stations and labels stay readable; the towns glow warm and their lit windows (listed when the land is painted) are filled over it. The airport lights its runway edges, taxiway and apron.
- **Speed.** A frame of the level 9 region measured 0.12x calibration before and after the restyle (the `region-looks` budget is 0.2x); by milliseconds, at noon and at night, it was 8–11% quicker at 1440×900 and 6–14% quicker on a phone slowed 4x than the flat map before it, because the land's hundreds of blocks and lines are one stamp.
- **Disruptions and weather.** `regionEvent` and `updateWeather` set their flags with `weather.set`, and the lines, roads and map read them with `weather.on`: see [Weather and events](weather.md).
- **Region news.** `news` (`28-region-weather.js`) is the Reports feed's Region news list. A signal failure or wire fault repeated on the same day folds into the one line that started it, naming every line and fault kind it's actually seen, rather than one near-identical line per incident.
