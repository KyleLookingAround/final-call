# The roof terrace

**The roof terrace** (`67-terrace.js`; spec `docs/specs/terminal-place.md`, step 5 of its order of work). A Terminal upgrade from level 3 (`G.lv.terrace`, `G.level>=2`), hidden before, in the layouts whose terminal has one: Classic for now, until the floor plans part gives each layout its own.
- **The room.** `ter` (`fl` 2) in Classic's `term` table (`classicTerm()`, `42-terminal.js`): cut into the roof over the concourse at the apron edge, between the left end of the building and stand A1, with `need:'terrace'` so it exists only once built. Two floor links on the market place's top wall, clear of its name: stairs (30 a minute) and a lift (8), which passengers queue for and ride like the escalators ([Floors](floors.md)); families and those who need help take the lift, runners never do and go ahead in the stairs' queue. `pub` is the **public side** beyond a glass screen, drawn but never walked. `terraceBox()` is both sides' box, `null` until it's built. On a layout without a `ter` room the upgrade is hidden (`UPG.terrace.tab` is `null`, and `req` fails), nobody is sent up and there are no spotters.
- **Open** (`R.terOpen`, worked out each minute) from 06:00 to 22:00 while it's dry: rain, snow or a storm (`weather.on`) closes the passengers' side, and anyone up or on the way comes down (`nextAct`).
- **Who goes up.** `nextAct`'s hook (`NEXT_ACT`, `46-market.js`) runs just before the market place would seat a waiting passenger: while it's open and has room (`TER_CAP`, 40 places), 1 in 6 (`TER_ODDS`; 1 in 4, `TER_WIDE`, while a wide-body is at a stand) goes up instead, and their party follows. One `rnd()` draw decides whether, and how long: 10–30 minutes. They walk to the stairs (`toTer`), and on the deck (`ter`) stand at one of forty places (`terSpot`: the rail, the benches, the café tables, the kiosk's queue) until their time is up or their gate is called (`toGate`, like shoppers). Nobody goes back up while still on the terrace. `terList()` (runtime) holds who is up or on the way, looked over each minute; it is also how the card and drawing find them without scanning `R.pax`.
- **Late runners.** A passenger on the terrace can dawdle there when their gate is called (`RUN_FROM.ter`, `61-late-runners.js`): they stay until final call sends them (`finalCall`), or `LINGER_MKT` minutes pass, and run down the stairs. Their story says so (`TALE_KIND`, `TALE_MORE`): "Up on the roof terrace", "Ran from the roof for gate 14", and if they miss, "Still on the roof when gate 14 closed" (or "Gate 14 closed on the run from the roof" if they were down by then). A miss costs the runners' −0.6 (`runner`), once.
- **Spotters** (`R.spot`, runtime, `terSpotters`) are a count, never passengers, and follow the hour and weather only: none from 23:00 to 05:00, one either side of that, then up to a steady 9–10 by day and back down after 20:00; a few die-hards in the wet (at most 30%). While a famous face is up (`R.terFam`), 6 photographers and 10 fans join them.
- **Famous faces** (`FAMOUS_MIN`, `64-famous-faces.js`): with the terrace open and the celebrity waiting in the market place with 40 minutes or more before their gate call, they go up for 10–20 minutes (one `rnd()` draw), once, to a place of their own at the glass; everyone else up packs towards that end.
- **Earnings,** together at most 2% of a day's income at level 9 (about 0.7% on the `terrace` check's day): the café kiosk earns `TER_CAFE` × (1 + 0.3 × the aircraft's tier) from about half of those who go up (by their own `rand`, not `rnd()`), 60% more while the famous face is up, as `'shops'` (`terraceCafe`); the public side pays `TER_FEE` a spotter-hour as `'landside'` (`terraceMinute`). Only these two functions earn for it, which is how the check tells its takings from the rest.
- **The rating** (`REPWHY.terrace`, `REPLBL.terrace`, "The roof terrace"): +0.01 for each passenger who reached the deck and then their gate (`boardedFromRoof`, looked for once a minute among those who went up, `R.terB`, rather than on every step of every walk to a gate), at most +0.4 a day as it lands (`R.terRep` keeps the day's total after `effect()`'s own scaling); −0.1 for each hour at capacity.
- **Saved state.** `G.lv.terrace` (0 or 1) and `G.terrace` (`FIELDS`: `{d, up, take}`, the day and today's passengers up, each counted once until they reach their gate, and takings for the card). Nobody is up in a loaded game: passengers aren't saved.
- **The card** (`TERM_PANEL.staff`, `#terrace`, Terminal › Staff) once built: today's passengers up, the spotters now, whether it's closed to passengers, today's takings and rating.
- **The advisor** (`terraceTip`, from `advise`, `16-advisor.js`): once a visit, from level 3, while 10 or more are standing in the market place and the terrace is affordable; it shows for up to six hours from the first time (`R.terTip`, runtime), then not again until the game is loaded afresh.
- **Drawing** (`drawTerrace`, on the `roofs` layer after the roofs, only on the Roof stop): timber decking (wet, snowy, darker at night), the rail with string lights at night, benches, café tables with parasols when it's dry by day, the kiosk, the stair head and lift, the glass screen, the public side's paving, telescope and lift; the passengers up (phones raised while a plane comes or goes, a gold ring on the famous face); at most 40 spotters (`R.spotDrawn`) and the famous face's crowd at the glass, with flashes at night or for the celebrity (cosmetic; none with reduced motion). Weather comes from `drawnFx()` and the dark from `V.d`, so photo mode dresses it. On the halls' floors the stairs and lift show upstairs only, and nothing of the terrace shows downstairs.
- **For the floor plans part.** The terrace lies over Classic's concourse (`main`), so the pending `plans: halls inside each main building` will list `ter` among halls overlapping an airside room until it leaves floor 2 rooms out; each layout's own plan places its own terrace.
- **Checks** (`tools/checks/terrace.mjs`, one seeded page for the day's checks): hidden until level 3, the card once bought; on the Roof at every zoom and on no other floor; passengers going up and coming down by its links, none left up after their call; runners down the stairs, boarding or with the roof in their story; a steady handful of spotters through the biggest arrival; fewer at night and in rain, and closed to passengers in the wet; spotters never in a queue and at most 40 drawn; small takings from both sources; its own rating line within +0.4; the famous face up and back before the gate call, with the fans; no toast or board line about it; the card at 320 px.
