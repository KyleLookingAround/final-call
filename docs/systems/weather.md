# Weather and events

**Weather and events** (`28-region-weather.js`, `10-events-toasts.js`; drawn in `54-weather.js`, check `weather-fx`). The weather cells that cross the region, and the flags in `R.fx` that say what's on now: the weather (fog, snow, rain, storm), the terminal's events (rush, sick, strike), fuel prices (hedge, fuelUp, fuelDown) and the region's disruptions (leaves, roadworks, and per line a fault and its replacement buses).
- **One place to set and read.** Each flag in `R.fx` is the game minute it lasts until. `weather.set(kind, until)` sets one and `weather.on(kind)` reads it, both in `28-region-weather.js`; a per-line flag takes the line's id as a third or second argument (`weather.set('repl', until, id)`, `weather.on('line', id)`). `weather.raise` sets a spell without shortening a longer one (the cells' fog and snow), `weather.until` gives the raw minute for the fades in `54-weather.js`, `weather.fault(id, until, why)` stops a line and keeps the reason the map shows (`weather.why(id)`), `weather.roadworks(until, edge)` closes a road edge (`weather.edge()`), and `weather.reset()` clears them all on load. It's called `weather`, not `wx`, because `wx` is the stand-to-world transform in `41-airside.js`. `R.fx` is runtime only: nothing here is saved.
- **Who sets what.** `updateWeather` (each step) turns on fog and snow while a cell is over the airport, and storm and rain while one is close enough (closer with the radar). `fireEvent` (`10-events-toasts.js`) sometimes hands over to `regionEvent` (a signal failure, a wire fault, roadworks or autumn leaves), and otherwise rolls rush, sick or strike; its fog and snow branches are never rolled today. Nothing sets hedge, fuelUp or fuelDown yet. The systems review's proposal 8 makes the cells the only source of weather, and this file is where it starts.
- **Still reading `R.fx` directly.** Two files, outside the briefs that moved the rest to `weather.on`; they keep working because `R.fx` keeps its shape: `07-passengers.js` (storm holds the runway) and `04-geometry.js` (`fuelMul`). The terminal's files (`39-layouts.js`'s `busMul`, `43-departures.js`'s bag drops and sick lane, `47-hotel.js`'s stranded passengers) moved in the release's terminal polish pass, leaving `PLAY` identical, as `12-drawing.js`'s snow on the runway and strike had before. The `weather-fx` check stays true through it.
- **The check.** `weather-fx` runs the level 9 save for a day and a bit and records each change to the set of flags that are on; the recording (`tools/checks/lib/weather-fx.json`) was made on `main` before the accessor. A change meant to alter the weather or the events re-records it with `WEATHER_FX_RECORD=1 node tools/check.mjs weather-fx` and says why in its PR.
