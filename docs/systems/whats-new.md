# What's new

**What's new** (`38-updates.js`). `UPDATES` holds every version's headline and points. The card opens after loading when `G.seen` is older than the newest version (not for new games or during the guided start) and from Settings or Help. Only a release adds an entry: a change players will notice leaves its points in its own file in `src/updates.d/` (no version number), and the `release` playbook gives the next version and folds them into one entry and one `docs/HISTORY.md` row, so two sessions never claim the same version. The `rules` check matches the newest entry against `docs/HISTORY.md`. A quiet Ko-fi link sits at its foot, below Play (`docs/specs/kofi-link.md`). A young save (Airfield or Local Airport) sees only its most recent entries; the rest fold behind one "N earlier versions" summary, since most of the older history describes systems it hasn't reached. Each point is `{b, t, go, lv}` (a plain string still works): a bold lead, one short sentence, an optional "Show me" target and an optional level. `newsOk` says which targets are real (the level-up card's `lvlGo` targets, `<tab>:<sub-tab>` from `NEWS_SUB`, `help` and `photo`), `newsCan` shows a button only once the save has that tab, and `newsGo` closes the card and goes there. Points above `G.level` are hidden; a version with any ends with a "N more for later levels · Show" control (`newsSpoil`) that reveals them in place for that viewing (`R.newsSpoil`, never saved), each tagged with its level, and a version with every point hidden shows its title and that control. The card is a header, a scrolling list (`#newsBody`, with the Ko-fi link at its foot) and a footer that always holds Play. The `news-card` check holds every point, released or waiting in `src/updates.d/`, to that shape, taps every "Show me" button and a Show control, and checks the phone fit.
