# What's new

**What's new** (`38-updates.js`). `UPDATES` holds every version's headline and points. The card opens after loading when `G.seen` is older than the newest version (not for new games or during the guided start) and from Settings or Help. Only a release adds an entry: a change players will notice leaves its points in its own file in `src/updates.d/` (no version number), and the `release` playbook gives the next version and folds them into one entry and one `docs/HISTORY.md` row, so two sessions never claim the same version. The `rules` check matches the newest entry against `docs/HISTORY.md`. A quiet Ko-fi link sits at its foot, below Play (`docs/specs/kofi-link.md`).
