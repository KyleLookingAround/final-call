# Windows

**Windows and watchers** (`68-windows.js`; spec `docs/specs/terminal-place.md`, step 4 of its order of work; check group `windows`). Glass along the terminal's apron walls, lit at night, and waiting passengers who walk to it to watch a wide-body go by. Drawing and a short walk only: no saved state and no rating. The glass and the choice of watchers draw no `rnd()`; the walk back to a seat in the market place draws it as any seat does (`goAct`, `toMkt`), so `PLAY` moves a little.
- **The glass** (`glass()`) is worked out from `ROOMS` for the layout as built, so it follows every layout and any later floor plan: each edge of a room that exists (`roomOn`, not `open`), airside (not `land`) and not the roof terrace (`fl` 2, which keeps its own rail), at least 10 units long, whose outside (8 units out from its middle) is in no room, above the halls (`SEC_Y`) and on the map. Each wall is cut into panes of at most 80 units, `[x1, y1, x2, y2, room, nx, ny]` with `(nx, ny)` pointing out to the apron. It is worked out again only when `ROOMS` or what's built changes. Classic has six walls (the concourse, the east hall and Pier B's three sides); the Round terminal has forty.
- **Drawing.** On the `terminal` layer, after the halls: a pale blue line along each pane on the floor of halls shown (`onFloor`'s rule: a room with no floor is on both), with a slow sheen moving along it by day from zoom 0.3 and mullions from 0.55; warm by night. Never on the Roof stop, where the terrace's rail is the only glass. At night `glassLights`, one entry in `LIGHTS`, stamps warm pools on the apron 14 units outside each pane (one cached glow, drawn additively by the lighting pass like the markings' lamps); on the Roof, the departures floor's pools.
- **Watchers.** Once a game minute (`TERM_MINUTE`, after the gate calls), `glassMinute` marks each stand with a wide-body (more than two seat blocks, not a freighter) being pushed back (`R.st[i].out`) or towed in (`inbound`) as worth watching until six minutes after it has gone (long enough to see it taxi away). For each, up to 16 passengers in all, nearest first, go to the panes within 220 units of the stand: those waiting in the market place or at a gate lounge (not children, dawdlers, the famous face or the play area), on the pane's floor, within 170 units of it, and free to watch (`winFree`: gate not called, plane not boarding, at least a minute from the call). They take state `watch` (`PAX_STEP.watch`, `p.watch` set), walk (`route`, `walk`) to a spot just inside the glass towards the plane, and stay. A gate-lounge passenger only uses glass in their own room and keeps their seat; one from the market place gives up their seat and finds another on the way back (`goAct`).
- **Never after the call.** The step sends a watcher back the moment their gate is called or their plane starts boarding, and the minute's look does so in the same minute as the call, so nobody watches once called: a market-place watcher goes straight to the gate (`toGate`), a gate-lounge one back to the lounge where boarding takes them. The gate lounges sit by the glass, so nobody misses a flight to watch.
- **Speed.** The simulation costs a scan of the stands each minute, and the candidates only while a wide-body moves; the step runs only for the few watching. Drawing is one path for the glass and one stamp a pane at night.
