# A roof terrace
Status: landed
Code: V36
Summary: Watch the planes from a terrace above the halls.

- A terrace at the apron edge that passengers go up to while they wait, to watch the planes and take photos.
- A public side for spotters with their cameras, and a passengers’ side with benches and a café kiosk.
- When someone famous flies through, fans and photographers crowd the glass.

Note: It arrives with level 3.
