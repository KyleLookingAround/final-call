Growing

Levels: hit the targets in Office › Plan to climb ten levels. Each level brings plan points.

Masterplan: press P. Spend plan points to approve plans that unlock aircraft, routes, upgrades, shops and transport. You can’t have everything at once, so choose what your airport needs.

Routes: press W for the world map. Each city has a market: open new routes when your flights start leaving emptier. Planes fly the best route within their range. Business cities pay more, holiday cities fill up in their season, and some attract groups.

Fleet: bigger jets reach further. More planes keep gates busy; partner airlines fill the gaps for a cut.

Region: press R for the map. Draw lines by tapping stations: buses, trams, trains, water buses, the metro and high-speed rail. Every line runs on its own, lines share track and stations, and riders change between them. Good links bring flyers, carry commuters, ease traffic and grow towns. Jobs fill business cities, tourism fills holiday cities, and high-speed rail feeds long-haul. Events need lines that can move the crowds.

Weather: fog, rain, snow and storms drift across the region map. Watch the forecast on the board: storms close the runway while they’re overhead.

Policies: Office › Policies sets standing orders: late passengers, connections, repairs, staff pay, advertising and a night curfew.

Tips: the bar at the bottom of the map names your bottleneck and the fix.

Recommendations and managers: the Region and Routes tabs suggest the lines, station upgrades, routes, fares and planes that would earn most, with one-tap Build, Open or Apply. A transport manager sets timetables and a route manager sets fares. Change anything yourself and they leave it to you; switch them off in Office › Settings when you want full control.

Settings: Office › Settings turns tips, messages, map pop-ups, recommendations, the goal bar, badges, managers and sound on or off.
