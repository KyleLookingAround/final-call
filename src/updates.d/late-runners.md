# Late runners, and passengers' stories

- Passengers still shopping at final call run for their gate. The gate holds briefly, the board shows GATE CLOSING, and anyone who misses it costs a little rating.
- Tap a passenger to read their day: where they came from, their queues and shops, and how they feel about it.
