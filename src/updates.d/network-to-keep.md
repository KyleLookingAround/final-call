# Routes you have to keep

- A route you rarely fly goes quiet: its travellers drift away, and its line fades on the world map. Lowmere moves in first.
- Partner airlines now fill the routes you fly least, so you'll see their flights to your cities on the board.
- Mark a route Keep on its card and your planes fly it at least that often. The route manager says which routes are slipping.

History: Routes need flying to keep their travellers. Partners fill the gaps, Lowmere moves in on neglected routes, and a new Keep setting guarantees a route's flights.
