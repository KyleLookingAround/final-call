# Routes you have to keep

- **Routes to keep.** A route you rarely fly goes quiet and its travellers drift away. (go: tab:routes · level: 1)
- **Partner airlines.** They fill the routes you fly least, so you’ll see them on the board. (level: 1)
- **Keep.** Mark a route Keep and your planes fly it at least that often. (go: tab:routes · level: 1)

History: Routes need flying to keep their travellers. Partners fill the gaps, Lowmere moves in on neglected routes, and a new Keep setting guarantees a route's flights.
