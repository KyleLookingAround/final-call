# Tidier gates, a smoother mover, and no more walking through walls

- Stand cards on the airport view no longer overlap and hide each other; a card that can't find a clear spot shows just its stand number.
- The Pier B people mover's car now glides along the track instead of snapping between the piers.
- Passengers no longer cut across a corner of a hall wall when catching up on screen; they always walk through the doorway.

History: Fixed three phone bugs the owner spotted at launch: overlapping stand cards, the people mover's car snapping between stops, and passengers occasionally cutting through a wall instead of a doorway.
