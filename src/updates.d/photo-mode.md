# Photo mode

- Photo mode hides every panel so you can frame your airport or the Region for a picture: the camera button at the top, or How to play on a phone.
- Pick the time of day and the weather for the picture: the game itself doesn't change.
- The shutter saves a picture, or opens the share sheet on a phone.

History: Photo mode: hide the panels, pick the time of day and the weather, and save or share a picture of your airport or the Region.
