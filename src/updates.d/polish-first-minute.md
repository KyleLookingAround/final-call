# Polish for the first minute on a phone

- The guided start's "tap gate A1" step no longer points at ground the map toolbar covers, and the toolbar's own background never blocks a tap meant for the map.
- Tab labels, the board's brand pill, the cash/rating/last-hour stats and the goal bar no longer clip or collide on the smallest phones, in portrait or landscape.
- Toasts and the advisor's tip no longer read as one jumble, and the buy, dismiss and link buttons across the game are easier to tap.
- Once your first flight is away, the advisor offers a one-off tip to try 4× speed if you're still watching at 1× or 2×.
