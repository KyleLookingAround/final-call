# A rating that shows how today is going

- Your rating now follows the last day of flights. Late departures, long queues and night noise pull it down within hours, and a good run lifts it back.
- An arrow beside the rating shows which way it's heading. Tap the rating to see what moved it.
- The top levels now ask you to keep a good day going, not just reach one.

History: The rating follows the last 24 hours instead of adding up for ever, so it moves with fog, delays and queues, with an arrow and its biggest cause beside it.
