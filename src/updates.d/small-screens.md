# Better on small screens

- **Phones on their side.** The top bar stays on one row, and the side panel fits its cash, stars and tabs.
- **Labels stay.** With six tabs or fewer, the tab names show even on a narrow phone.
- **More room.** Open the panel fully on a small phone and the board steps aside.
- **Help.** Shorter, and without keyboard wording on touch screens.

History: Small screens got a pass: the top bar and side panel fit a phone on its side, tab labels stay with six tabs or fewer, the bottom sheet keeps room for the panel when fully open, board columns are wider, and Help is shorter and drops keyboard wording on touch screens.
