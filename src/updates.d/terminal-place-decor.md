# Decor and local names

- **Halls with character.** Planters, benches, murals and a departures board arrive as you grow.
- **Local names.** Some cafés, shops, murals and the terrace café are named after the region.

History: Every hall of the terminal fills with planters, benches, murals and a grand departures board as the airport grows, never in anyone's way, and some shops, cafés, the murals and the roof terrace's café take their names and colours from the region's towns.
