# The roof terrace

- **A roof terrace.** Waiting passengers watch the planes from the roof, with spotters beside them. (go: terminal:staff · level: 3)

History: A roof terrace over the concourse, a Terminal upgrade from level 3: waiting passengers go up the stairs to watch the planes and get a coffee, spotters gather on the public side, famous faces go up too, and it closes to passengers in the wet and at night. It shows on the Roof stop.
