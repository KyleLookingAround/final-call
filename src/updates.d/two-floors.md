# Two floors in Classic

- **Two floors.** Departures upstairs, arrivals below: pick a floor on the camera bar.
- **Escalators and a lift.** Arrivals ride down; families take the lift, and busy escalators queue.
- **Tap a hall's name.** The camera flies there and shows that hall's floor.

History: Classic's terminal now stands on two floors, departures upstairs and arrivals below, with escalators and a lift between them. Tapping a hall's name, a tip or a board line goes to its floor. The other layouts keep one floor for now.
