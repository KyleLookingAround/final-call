# A What's new you can act on

- **Shorter points.** Each change is a bold lead and one short line.
- **Show me.** A button on a point takes you straight to what changed.
- **Only what you've reached.** Points for later levels wait until you get there.

History: What's new is shorter and scans quickly, with Show me buttons that go to each change, points for later levels hidden until you reach them, and Play always in reach on phones.
